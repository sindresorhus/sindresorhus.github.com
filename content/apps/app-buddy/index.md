Export and import the settings of [my apps](/apps) to back them up or move them to another Mac, reset their privacy permissions, and more.

It’s a separate app because some features are not possible in sandboxed apps, which most of my apps are.

I welcome suggestions for what more useful support tools it could contain.

*I also have future plans to make this app provide extra functionality for my sandboxed apps.*

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Can it support apps other than yours? {#can-it-support-apps-other-than-yours}

No. This is intended for my apps only.

If you need app setting import/export for any apps, check out my [Supercharge](/supercharge) app.
