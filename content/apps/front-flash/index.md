Take selfies in the dark with the front camera. Front Flash turns the screen white at full brightness while it takes the picture, like a flash.

- Take self-portraits in low light with the front camera
- Tap anywhere on the screen to take a picture
- Use it as a mirror
- Share with your friends

It was my first iPhone app, released on November 17, 2013. In March 2014, Apple featured it when the App Store got a new section for selfie apps, as [Engadget](https://archive.ph/16L4g) and [Cult of Mac](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317235818/http://www.cultofmac.com:80/269985/app-store-now-section-devoted-selfie-apps) reported. See its [App Store page](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/vxlxo3j6uzlpke4n33kpw/app-store-page-1791870683.jpg?rlkey=n05i3ulj2cqg744az8nmb7i6p&raw=1) from back then.

It was free at first, and later $2. It had about 40,000 downloads, and it earned around $10,000. Its App Store page had almost a million views.

In 2015, Apple [sherlocked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_(software)#Sherlocked_as_a_term) it: the iPhone 6s got [Retina Flash](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2015/09/09Apple-Introduces-iPhone-6s-iPhone-6s-Plus/), which lights up the screen for selfies with the built-in Camera app. The app was removed from the App Store on May 25, 2018.
