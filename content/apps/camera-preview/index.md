Check your hair and lighting before a video call. Open the app to see your Mac’s camera, and press ++Return++ to take a photo.

**Features**

- Show the built-in camera or an external camera
- Optionally show the app in the menu bar, and show and hide the camera with a keyboard shortcut or by clicking the notch
- Keep the camera window on top of other windows
- Flip the image or zoom in
- Take photos as JPEG or PNG, or copy them directly
- Choose the folder that photos are saved in
- Show the camera and change the zoom from Shortcuts

## Tips

- Press the ++Return++ or ++Space++ key while showing the camera feed to take a photo.
- Press ++Command+Plus++ to zoom in, ++Command+-++ to zoom out, and ++Command+0++ to reset the zoom.
- Press the ++Option++ key while resizing the window to resize from the center (this works with any app).

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### The app does not work {#not-working}

- Ensure you are on the latest version.
- Restart your computer.
- Try [resetting permissions](/apps/faq#mac-reset-permissions).

### Can you add support for making the camera feed a circle? {#can-you-add-support-for-making-the-camera-feed-a-circle}

I don’t plan to add that. I plan to make a separate app specifically for showing the camera on presentations and screencasts, which will have this feature.

### Can it float over fullscreen apps? {#can-it-float-over-fullscreen-apps}

Yes, when in menu bar mode.

### How is this better than Photo Booth? {#photo-booth}

- Optional access from the menu bar, with a keyboard shortcut to show and hide it.
- Higher quality camera output.
- Does not crop the camera output.
- Minimalistic design with no window frame.
- Takes photos without the countdown.
- Photos are saved to the file system, not hidden inside the app.
- The window can be made to always stay on top of other windows.
- Capture and copy photo in a single action.
- Save photo as lossless PNG file.
- Includes camera metadata (EXIF) in the saved photo.
- Made by an indie developer that listens to users.

### How is this different from Hand Mirror? {#hand-mirror}

Hand Mirror is a great app, and it is free. Its paid upgrade, Hand Mirror Plus, adds features like a microphone level check, window masks, and Polaroid-style photos. If you want those, use Hand Mirror.

Camera Preview has all its features in one purchase, with a focus on a camera window you can keep open and take photos from:

- Photos are saved as JPEG or PNG files with camera metadata (EXIF), and you can copy a photo directly.
- The window can stay on top of other windows.
- Flip the image horizontally or vertically.
- Show the camera and change the zoom from Shortcuts.

### How is this better than QuickTime? {#quicktime}

- Shows the camera faster. One click instead of multiple.
- Optional access from the menu bar, with a keyboard shortcut to show and hide it.
- Remembers window size and position.
- Takes photos.
- The window can be made to always stay on top of other windows.
- Capture and copy photo in a single action.

### What resources are used when the app is in the menu bar? {#what-resources-are-used-when-the-app-is-in-the-menu-bar}

When the camera window is closed or minimized, the camera is turned off. The app consumes approximately 40 MB of RAM and 0% of CPU when running in the background.

### Do you plan to add background removal? {#do-you-plan-to-add-background-removal}

I don’t have any immediate plans for this. Maybe in a year when AI background removal has progressed further.

### Do you plan to add filters? {#do-you-plan-to-add-filters}

No.

### Can it record video? {#can-it-record-video}

No. That’s outside the scope of this app. You can use the built-in QuickTime Player app for that.
