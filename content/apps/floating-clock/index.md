Display a digital clock anywhere in your space.

The app is simple by design.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### How is this different from the Clock widget in visionOS 26? {#how-is-this-different-from-the-clock-widget-in-visionos-26}

Since visionOS 26, the built-in Clock app has a widget that you can put on a wall or another flat surface, where it stays. If that is what you want, use it.

Floating Clock is a window, so you can put it anywhere in your space, also in the air in front of you. It shows a simple digital clock, and you can choose to show the date and the background. Tap the clock to change the settings, and choose “New Window” there to add another clock.

### Can it support showing an analog clock? {#can-it-support-showing-an-analog-clock}

No plans to support that.

### Can it show seconds? {#can-it-show-seconds}

No plans to support that.

### Can it show the year? {#can-it-show-the-year}

The year is omitted for simplicity; it’s generally known.

### Can you support macOS? {#can-you-support-macos}

I plan to do this at some point, but I cannot say when.

### Can you localize the app into my language? {#can-you-localize-the-app-into-my-language}

I don’t plan to localize the app, but the time and date respect your locale.
