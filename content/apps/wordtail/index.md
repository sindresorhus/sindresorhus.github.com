Make words on a 15×15 board against a lemur, by yourself, or with a friend. Learn the meanings of the words you meet along the way.

**Features**

- Play against the lemur at four levels, from Easy to Expert
- Solo games, Pass & Play on one device, and online games with friends through Game Center
- A daily puzzle, the same for everyone: find the best play with one move
- English and Norwegian Bokmål words (the interface is in English)
- See the meanings of words on the board, save words, and review them later
- What Could I Have Played? shows a better move after your turn
- Strict Word Check, for games where invalid words lose the turn
- Hardware keyboard support
- Works offline, except for online games. No accounts, ads, or analytics.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### How do I see the meaning of a word on the board?

Touch and hold a played tile, then choose “Show Meanings”. It shows the words through that tile, across and down. To see every word on the board, choose “Words on Board” in the ⋯ menu. Turn History in the same menu shows the words of each turn.

A tap on a played tile does nothing, since taps meant for placing a tile or zooming often land on one.
