Classic sudoku on a clean, full-width board. No mistake limit, no streaks, and no notifications. Just you and the puzzle.

**Features**

- Five difficulty levels, from Easy to Extreme, all solvable by logic, without guessing
- A daily puzzle, the same for everyone, with an archive of earlier days
- Notes, Fill Notes, Clear Notes, undo, and highlighting of the selected digit
- Repeated digits show in red
- Scan a printed puzzle with the camera, or choose a photo, and play it
- Share a puzzle with a friend as a link
- Statistics for each difficulty, with a chart of recent solve times
- A timer that you can hide
- Apple Pencil on iPad: write digits with Scribble, scratch them out to erase, and double-tap or squeeze to turn Notes on or off
- Hardware keyboard support
