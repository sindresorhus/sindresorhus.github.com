Open the app and start drawing. For quick sketches, brainstorming, and doodles, without setting anything up first.

The app is intentionally simple.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### I can already access these drawing tools in the Notes and Freeform app {#i-can-already-access-these-drawing-tools-in-the-notes-and-freeform-app}

This app makes it faster to get started. Just tap the app and start doodling. It also lets you save or erase more quickly. Great for quick brainstorm sessions.

### How can I undo? {#how-can-i-undo}

Tap the undo button, shake the device, or swipe left with three fingers.

### Can I zoom in and out? {#can-i-zoom-in-and-out}

There is no zoom functionality. The app is designed for straightforward, full-canvas doodling.

### Can I draw on an existing image? {#can-i-draw-on-an-existing-image}

This is not supported.

### Can you support shape detection? {#can-you-support-shape-detection}

Apple does not let third-party apps use the shape detection functionality, so this is not possible.

### Why can I only use a single hand when drawing on the visionOS version? {#why-can-i-only-use-a-single-hand-when-drawing-on-the-visionos-version}

This is a visionOS bug.

### Can you support macOS? {#can-you-support-macos}

Unfortunately not possible as the framework used for drawing is too limited on macOS.
