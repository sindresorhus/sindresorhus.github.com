Quickly open websites, launch apps, and run shortcuts directly from your Lock Screen.

For example, open google.com, launch TikTok, or run a shortcut to play a random music playlist.

Here are [some more ideas](https://github.com/bhagyas/app-urls) of what you could open.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### How do I add the widget to the Lock Screen? {#how-do-i-add-the-widget-to-the-lock-screen}

First, make a widget preset in the app. Then, on the Lock Screen, tap and hold on the clock, tap the “Customize” button, tap the widget area below the clock, and select “Quick Launch”. Add the “Open Stuff” widget, tap it, and choose a preset.

To leave an empty space between widgets, for example to align them, add the “Spacer” widget.

[Learn more about Lock Screen widgets](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph4d0e6c351/ios#iph0ee454f4c)

### The widget does not open the app {#open-app}

Quick Launch opens many apps directly. For the other apps, it uses the “Quick Launch - Open App” shortcut. Tap “Add Shortcut” in the widget preset to add it to the Shortcuts app. You only need to do this once.

Write the app name exactly as it shows on the Home Screen. Use the “Open App” button in the widget preset to test it.

### Can you support more actions when tapping a widget? {#can-you-support-more-actions-when-tapping-a-widget}

Sure. I’m happy to consider requests. [Send it here.](/feedback?product=Quick%20Launch&referrer=Website-FAQ)

### How does it compare to other similar Lock Screen launchers? {#how-does-it-compare-to-other-similar-lock-screen-launchers}

It can open any app, not just from a predefined list, and it has more icon choices.

### How is this different from the Lock Screen controls? {#how-is-this-different-from-the-lock-screen-controls}

You can replace the flashlight and camera buttons at the bottom of the Lock Screen with controls, like “Open App” or a shortcut. If two buttons are enough for you, use them.

Quick Launch uses the widget area below the clock instead, so you can have up to four buttons in addition to the two controls. It can also open a website directly, and you can choose the icon of each button: a symbol or up to two characters.
