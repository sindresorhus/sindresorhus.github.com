## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Can macOS do this without an app? {#built-in}

Partly. The Calendar app can show week numbers: in Calendar, go to “Settings › Advanced”, and enable “Show week numbers”. macOS cannot show the week number in the menu bar.

Week Number shows it in the menu bar, where you can always see it, and it can find the week number of any date.

### Can you add custom week numbering options (for example, fiscal weeks, school terms, or work weeks)? {#can-you-add-custom-week-numbering-options-for-example-fiscal-weeks-school-terms-or-work-weeks}

No, the app is intentionally simple.
