A shopping list that is fast to add to and fast to check off.

Type an item and press return. It goes into the right store section automatically, like Produce, Dairy, or Household, so your list follows the way you walk through the store. Tap an item to check it off.

The app is intentionally simple. No accounts, settings, ads, recipes, meal plans, or prices. Nothing to set up. It does one thing well: your shopping list.

Available in English and Norwegian. I may do more languages, but no promises.

**Highlights**

- Sorted by store section automatically, with Apple Intelligence
- Share a list with your family and see their changes right away
- Check off items from a widget, without opening the app

**More features**

- Suggestions as you type, from your earlier items and common groceries
- Type “2 milk” to set the quantity
- Multiple lists, for example, one for each store
- The screen stays on while you shop
- Send a list as text to someone without an iPhone
- Syncs across your devices with iCloud
- Paste a copied list to add one item per line
- Lock Screen widgets, a Control Center control, and the Action button
- Siri and Shortcuts: “Add to Bare”
- Apple Watch app that works on its own

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### An item is in the wrong section {#wrong-category}
<!-- @faq.keywords category categories -->

Touch and hold the item, tap “Category”, and pick another one. The app remembers it, so the next time you add an item with the same name, it goes in the same section.

### How does the app know which store section an item goes in? {#categorization}
<!-- @faq.keywords category categories categorize aisle sorting -->

In this order:

1. The section you picked for an item with the same name.
2. The section an earlier item with the same name got.
3. A built-in list of common groceries.
4. Apple Intelligence, with Private Cloud Compute or the on-device model. The item is added right away, and the section is set a moment later.

### How do I share a list with my family? {#share}
<!-- @faq.keywords partner household invite -->

Open the list, tap its name at the top, and then “Share List”. The people you invite need an iCloud account and an iPhone, iPad, or Mac with the app. Each list is shared on its own, so you can keep some lists private.

To send the list to someone without an iPhone, use “Send as Text” in the same menu. They get the list as text, sorted by section.

### How do I join a list someone shared with me? {#join}
<!-- @faq.keywords invitation accept -->

Open the invitation link on a device with the app. The app shows “Joining List…” until the list appears. You need to be signed in to iCloud. If it fails, tap “Try Again”.

### iCloud syncing does not work {#icloud-sync}

[Troubleshooting.](/apps/faq#icloud-sync)

### How do I set the quantity? {#quantity}

Type it with the name, like “2 milk”, “2x milk”, or “milk x2”. Text like “500 g flour” stays in the name.

You can also swipe right on an item to add or remove one. If you add an item that is already on the list, its quantity increases instead of adding it twice.

### How do I edit an item? {#edit}
<!-- @faq.keywords notes -->

Touch and hold the item and tap “Edit”, or swipe left on it and tap the pencil button. There you can change the name, quantity, category, and notes. On Apple Watch, you can see notes but not edit them.

### Where do checked items go? {#done}
<!-- @faq.keywords completed disappear -->

They move to Done at the bottom of the list. Tap an item there to put it back on the list. To remove them, tap the trash button in the Done header.

### Can I hide the Done section? {#hide-done}
<!-- @faq.keywords collapse -->

Yes. Tap “Done” to collapse it, and tap it again to show the items.

### How do I use the same list again? {#reuse-list}
<!-- @faq.keywords template recurring weekly reset reuse -->

Tap the “…” menu and then “Uncheck All”. All the items in Done go back on the list.

### Does it work without Apple Intelligence? {#without-apple-intelligence}

Yes. Common groceries are still sorted, and other items go in Other. See [which devices have Apple Intelligence](https://support.apple.com/121115). You can move an item to another section yourself, and the app remembers it.

### Why is an item in Other? {#other-section}

The app could not find a section for it, or it could not sort it yet, for example, when you added it on Apple Watch while offline. Touch and hold the item, tap “Category”, and pick one. The app remembers it for the next time.

### Can I change the order of the sections to match my store? {#section-order}
<!-- @faq.keywords aisle layout -->

No. The sections are in the order most stores use, starting with Produce. Custom orders and custom categories will not be supported, as they need setup, and the app is designed to work without any.

### Does the Apple Watch app need my iPhone? {#watch}

No. It syncs directly with iCloud, so it also works when your iPhone is at home. Items you add on the watch are sorted with Private Cloud Compute. If it is not available, for example, offline, they are sorted the next time you open the app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

### The widget does not show my latest changes {#widget-not-updating}
<!-- @faq.keywords outdated stale refresh -->

The widget gets its data from the app. If you force-quit the app, the widget may not update until you open the app again. You do not need to quit apps on iOS.

### Does it work without an internet connection? {#offline}
<!-- @faq.keywords signal coverage -->

Yes. Your lists are on your device, so you can add and check off items without a connection, for example, in a store with bad coverage. The changes sync when you are online again.

### How do I add many items at once? {#paste}
<!-- @faq.keywords import bulk multiple copy -->

Copy a list with one item per line, for example, from Notes or a message. Then use “Paste” in the “…” menu of the list.

### Can I import my list from Reminders or another app? {#import}
<!-- @faq.keywords anylist bring ourgroceries -->

Yes. Copy the items as text, with one item per line, and then use “Paste” in the “…” menu of a list. Bullets like “- ” are removed.

### Can I change the order of the items? {#item-order}
<!-- @faq.keywords alphabetical drag -->

No. Items are sorted A to Z in each section, so they are easy to find. Done shows the most recently checked item first.

### How do I move an item to another list? {#move-item}

Touch and hold the item, and then tap “Move to List”. If the other list already has an unchecked item with the same name, the quantities are added together.

### Can I add details like the brand or size? {#notes}

Yes. Touch and hold the item, tap “Edit”, and write it in “Notes”. Keep the name short, like “Milk”, so the app can sort it and suggest it again.

### How can I add an item with Siri? {#siri}

Say “Add to Bare” or “Add to Groceries in Bare”, and then say the item. The app also has actions in the Shortcuts app.

### Can I undo? {#undo}

Yes, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Shake the iPhone, swipe left with three fingers, or press ++Command+Z++ on a keyboard. You can undo checking off, deleting, and “Uncheck All”. On Apple Watch, you can put an item back from Done, but you cannot undo a delete.

### How do I stop sharing or leave a shared list? {#stop-sharing}
<!-- @faq.keywords unshare remove -->

Open the list, tap its name at the top, and then “Manage Sharing”. There, the owner can remove people or stop sharing, and other people can leave the list. You can also swipe left on the list in the lists screen and tap “Leave”.

### Can the people I share with use Android or a computer? {#android}
<!-- @faq.keywords windows web browser -->

They need an iPhone, iPad, or Mac with the app. For someone else, use “Send as Text” to send them the list.

### Is my data private? {#privacy}

Your lists are stored on your devices and in your iCloud account. I cannot see them. Categorization uses Apple Intelligence on your device or Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, which does not store your data.

### Is there a Mac app? {#mac}

Not a native one, but the iPad app runs on Mac computers with Apple silicon.

### How do I remove a suggestion? {#remove-suggestion}
<!-- @faq.keywords history typo -->

Touch and hold the suggestion above the keyboard, and then tap “Remove Suggestion”. You can only remove suggestions from your earlier items, not the built-in common groceries.

### Why are the suggestions in the wrong language? {#suggestion-language}
<!-- @faq.keywords english norwegian -->

The built-in suggestions follow the language of the app. To change it, go to “Settings › Apps › Bare › Language”. Suggestions from your earlier items are always shown, in any language.

### Why do I get suggestions I never typed? {#common-suggestions}

The app has a built-in list of common groceries, about 5,000 in English and 3,900 in Norwegian. You cannot remove these, but your own items are suggested first.

### Why does the screen not turn off? {#screen-on}
<!-- @faq.keywords lock sleep battery -->

The screen stays on while a list is open, so the phone does not lock between aisles. Normal auto-lock comes back after ten minutes without changes.

### How do I turn off the haptic feedback? {#haptics}

The app follows the system setting: “Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics”.

### Where are the settings? {#settings}
<!-- @faq.keywords options -->

There are none. The app is designed to work well without setup. It follows the system settings, like “System Haptics” and the app language in “Settings › Apps › Bare”.

### How do I add items on Apple Watch? {#watch-add}

Open a list and tap the “+” button at the bottom. You can then dictate, type, or scribble the item. You can also add a list to a watch face as a complication.

### The watch goes back to the clock while I shop {#watch-return-to-clock}
<!-- @faq.keywords wrist face screen -->

On the watch, open Settings › General › Return to Clock, tap Bare, and turn on “Return to App”. The list then stays open when you raise your wrist.

### How do I choose which list the widget shows? {#widget-list}
<!-- @faq.keywords configure -->

Touch and hold the widget, tap “Edit Widget”, and choose a list. If that list is deleted, the widget shows your first list.

### How do I add an item with the Action button or Control Center? {#action-button}
<!-- @faq.keywords lock -->

Add the “Add Item” control. For the Action button, go to “Settings › Action Button”, and choose “Controls” and then “Add Item”. For Control Center, open it, tap “+”, and then “Add a Control”. You can choose which list the control adds to.

On Apple Watch, add the “Shopping List” control to Control Center. It opens the list, where you tap “+” to add an item.

### Are there keyboard shortcuts? {#keyboard-shortcuts}

Yes, on iPad and Mac:

- ++Command+N++: Add an item
- ++Shift+Command+N++: Make a new list
- ++Command+Z++: Undo

### How do I rename or delete a list? {#rename-delete-list}

In the lists screen, touch and hold the list. To rename the open list, you can also tap its name at the top.

### What happens when the owner deletes a shared list? {#shared-list-deleted}
<!-- @faq.keywords disappear -->

It is deleted for everyone. Changes that another person made and that were not synced yet are lost.

### Can I share all my lists at once? {#share-all-lists}
<!-- @faq.keywords household multiple -->

No. Each list is shared on its own. This lets you share the groceries with your family and keep other lists private.

### Can I see who added or checked off an item? {#attribution}

No. The app keeps the list simple and does not show who did what.

### Do I get a notification when someone adds an item? {#notifications}
<!-- @faq.keywords notify -->

No. The app does not send notifications. The list and the widgets update by themselves.

### We added the same item at the same time, and now it is there twice {#duplicate-items}
<!-- @faq.keywords duplicate -->

This can happen when two devices add a new item at the same moment, before they sync. Delete one of them.

### Can I transfer a shared list to someone else? {#transfer-ownership}
<!-- @faq.keywords owner -->

No. This is a limitation of iCloud sharing.

### Can I use the app without iCloud? {#without-icloud}

Yes. Your lists work on the device. To sync and share lists, sign in to iCloud in Settings.

### The app says the iCloud account changed {#account-changed}
<!-- @faq.keywords signed switched -->

The lists on the device belong to another iCloud account, so the app pauses syncing to not mix them. To use the current account, tap “Delete Lists on This Device”. Lists in iCloud are not affected, and the lists of the current account are downloaded. Changes that were not uploaded yet are lost.

### The app says the iCloud data was deleted {#icloud-data-deleted}

The app’s data in iCloud was deleted, for example, in Settings. Your lists are still on the device. Tap “Upload Lists Again” to keep them. Shared lists must be shared again.

### My iCloud storage is full {#icloud-storage-full}
<!-- @faq.keywords quota -->

Your changes stay on the device and sync when there is space again. The app shows a note about it in the “…” menu.

### What happens if I delete the app? {#delete-app}
<!-- @faq.keywords reinstall uninstall -->

Lists that synced to iCloud come back when you install the app again. Lists that did not sync, for example, without iCloud, are lost.

### Does it work with Alexa or Google Assistant? {#alexa}

No. You can use Siri.

### Does it support Assistive Access? {#assistive-access}
<!-- @faq.keywords accessibility -->

Yes. The app also supports VoiceOver and Dynamic Type.

### Can you add prices, photos, barcode scanning, recipes, or meal plans?

No. The app is intentionally simple, and these features would make it slower to use for most people.
