@QuickAnswer {
	In Voice Memos, select the recording, and click the Transcription button to see the text. To copy it, select the text, ++Control++-click it, and choose “Copy”. For other languages, subtitles, or a text file, drag the recording to the window of [Aiko](/aiko).
}

## With Aiko

@Steps {
1. Open Voice Memos and [Aiko](/aiko).
1. Drag the recording from the list of Voice Memos to the Aiko window.
1. Wait while Aiko transcribes. A long recording takes some minutes.
1. Click the export button in the toolbar, and choose “Text”, “Text with Timestamps”, or “SRT (Subtitles)”. Or copy the text.
}

If Aiko quits when you drop the recording, share the recording from Voice Memos to Aiko instead. [More in the FAQ.](/aiko#how-can-i-transcribe-audio-from-the-voice-memos-app)

Aiko uses the Whisper model of OpenAI on your Mac, so your audio is never uploaded. It finds the language itself, and it can also translate the speech to English. Try it free for 14 days with [the trial](/aiko#trial). On iPhone, see [how to transcribe a voice memo on iPhone](guide:transcribe-voice-memo-iphone).

## Without an app

Voice Memos shows a transcript of the recordings that have speech:

@Steps {
1. Open Voice Memos, and select the recording.
1. Click the Transcription button.
1. To copy the text, select it, ++Control++-click it, and choose “Copy”.
}

To find a recording by what is said in it, type a word in the search field. To see the text while you record, click the Transcription button during the recording. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/voice-memos/vm4a03609f0d/mac>

Audio transcription is not available in all countries or regions. For a memo in a language that Voice Memos transcribes, it is all you need. Aiko is for the other languages and for subtitles.
