@QuickAnswer {
	In the “Events” settings of [Dato](/dato), turn on “Upcoming event & reminder in menu bar”. The menu bar then shows the title of your next event and the time until it starts. macOS has no menu bar item for events. The “Up Next” widget of the Calendar app shows your next events on the desktop.
}

## With Dato

@Steps {
1. Open [Dato](/dato). It shows the events of the Calendar app, so add your Google or Outlook account there first, if it is not there.
1. Open the settings of Dato, and click “Events”.
1. In the “Notifications” section, turn on “Upcoming event & reminder in menu bar”. The first time, its settings open. Later, click “Customize…” to open them.
1. To show only meetings, turn on “Show for video call events only”. To join the call when you click the item, turn on “Join video call by clicking menu bar item”.
}

By default, the item shows an event when it starts in the next hour. Change this in “Show items starting in the next”. In “Show time as”, choose “Countdown”, “Start time”, or “None”. All-day events do not show.

@Tips {
- To join the next call from any app, set a keyboard shortcut for “Join video call” in the “Shortcuts” settings. It joins a call that is in progress or that starts in the next 15 minutes.
- To hide an event from the menu bar, right-click the item, and choose “Dismiss”. “Mute Event” also hides the event from the other notifications of Dato.
- If the item does not show, the menu bar can be full. Hold ++Command++, and drag the item nearer to the right side of the menu bar.
- For a reminder that you cannot miss, Dato can also cover the screen when the meeting starts. [How to get a fullscreen meeting reminder.](guide:fullscreen-meeting-reminder-mac)
}

## Without an app

The Calendar app shows your next events in a widget, and an alert before each event:

@Steps {
1. ++Control++-click the wallpaper, and choose “Edit Widgets…”.
1. Search for “Calendar”, and add the “Up Next” widget.
1. In the Calendar app, give your events an alert, like 5 minutes before.
}

The widget is on the desktop, so windows often cover it. The menu bar is always visible, also in a fullscreen app.
