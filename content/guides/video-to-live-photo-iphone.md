@QuickAnswer {
	The Photos app cannot turn a video into a Live Photo. Make a shortcut with the “Make Live Photo from Video” action of [Actions](../apps/actions/index.md), a free app, and it saves the Live Photo in your library.
}

## With Actions

@Steps {
1. Install [Actions](../apps/actions/index.md), and open it once.
1. In the Shortcuts app, tap the add button to make a new shortcut.
1. Add the “Select Photos” action.
1. Add the “Make Live Photo from Video” action of Actions. It gets the selected video. To use another frame than the first as the still image, tap the arrow in the action, and enter the time in seconds.
1. Run the shortcut, and pick a video. The Live Photo is saved in Photos.
}

It keeps the frame rate, the location, and the date of the video, and does not compress it. It takes MP4 and MOV videos. A shortcut action on iPhone only gets 30 seconds to run, so a very long video may not work. If the actions do not show in Shortcuts, restart your iPhone.

A Live Photo made from a video does not move when you use it as a Lock Screen wallpaper. This is a limitation of iOS.

## Without an app

iPhone has no built-in way to turn a video into a Live Photo. Instead, take a Live Photo with the Camera app: it records 1.5 seconds before and after you take the picture. [Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/104966)

The Loop and Bounce effects of Photos go the other way: they turn a Live Photo into a short video. The shortcut is for a video that you already have.
