@QuickAnswer {
	Press ++Command+[++ to go back and ++Command+]++ to go forward, in Safari and in Finder. The ++⌫++ key does not go back. To go back with ++⌫++, like in File Explorer on Windows, use [Supercharge](/supercharge).
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Tweaks”.
1. **Safari:** In the “Safari” section, turn on “Use Delete and ++Shift+Delete++ to navigate back and forward in Safari”. Supercharge asks for Full Disk Access, which it needs to change the setting of Safari. Restart Safari when it asks.
1. **Finder:** In the “Finder” section, turn on “Use ⌫ (Delete) to go back and ++Shift+⌫++ to go forward”.
1. Press ++⌫++ to go back, and ++Shift+⌫++ to go forward.
}

The Finder setting cannot be on together with the setting that [moves files to the Trash with ++⌫++](guide:delete-files-delete-key-mac). It also works with the Backspace key of external keyboards.

## Without an app

- **Keyboard:** Press ++Command+[++ to go back, and ++Command+]++ to go forward. This works in Safari, in Finder, and in most browsers.
- **Trackpad:** In Safari, swipe left or right with two fingers.
- **Toolbar:** Click the back and forward buttons.

[Apple’s keyboard shortcuts.](https://support.apple.com/102650) ++Command+[++ is the way of the Mac, and it is good to learn. Supercharge is for when your fingers expect ++⌫++, like when you also use Windows. To go to the folder above the current folder, not back, see [how to go up one folder in Finder](guide:go-up-folder-finder-mac).
