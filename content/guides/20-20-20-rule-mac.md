@QuickAnswer {
	In the free [Pandan](/pandan) app, open the “Notifications” settings, and set “Show a gentle reminder” to “every 20 minutes”. When the reminder shows, look at something 20 feet (6 meters) away for 20 seconds. [Without an app](#without-an-app), start a 20 minute timer in the Clock app, as macOS has no eye break reminder.
}

The 20-20-20 rule is a common tip against tired eyes: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

## With Pandan

@Steps {
1. Open [Pandan](/pandan). It shows in the menu bar how long you have used your Mac.
1. Open its settings, click “Notifications”, and set “Show a gentle reminder” to “every 20 minutes”.
1. In “Type”, choose “HUD”, which shows the reminder in the middle of all screens, so you do not miss it.
}

Pandan counts only the time that you use the Mac. When you are away for a longer time, the session ends, and the count starts again when you come back.

To know when the 20 seconds are over, make a shortcut in the Shortcuts app with the “Wait” action set to 20 seconds, and the “Play Sound” action after it. Then choose the shortcut in “Run shortcut” in the “Notifications” settings of Pandan. [More about Pandan as an eye break reminder.](/pandan#eye-break-reminder)

## Without an app

macOS has no eye break reminder. The Clock app can remind you one time:

@Steps {
1. Open the Clock app, and choose “View › Timers”.
1. Set 20 minutes, and click “Start”.
}

A notification shows when the timer ends. You must start the timer again after each break. Pandan repeats the reminder by itself, and does not count the time that you are away from the Mac.
