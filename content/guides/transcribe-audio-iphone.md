@QuickAnswer {
	In [Aiko](/aiko), tap the add button, choose “Import File…”, and choose the audio file. Aiko writes the text on your device, in about 100 languages. [Without an app](#without-an-app), use the “Transcribe Audio” action of the Shortcuts app, or see the transcript of a recording that you make in Voice Memos or Notes.
}

## With Aiko

@Steps {
1. Open [Aiko](/aiko), tap the add button, and choose “Import File…”.
1. Choose the audio file, like an M4A, MP3, or WAV file. Aiko starts to transcribe.
1. Keep Aiko open until it is done, as it cannot transcribe in the background. A long recording takes some minutes.
1. Tap the copy button to copy the text, or the export button to save it as text or subtitles, like “SRT (Subtitles)”.
}

You can also share an audio file from another app to Aiko. Aiko finds the language itself. If it picks the wrong one, choose it in “Audio language” in the settings. The transcription runs on your device, so your audio is never uploaded.

For a recording of Voice Memos, see [how to transcribe a voice memo on iPhone](guide:transcribe-voice-memo-iphone). For a video, see [how to transcribe a video on iPhone](guide:transcribe-video-iphone).

## Without an app

- **Shortcuts:** The “Transcribe Audio” action of the Shortcuts app transcribes an audio file. [How to transcribe audio in Shortcuts.](guide:transcribe-audio-shortcuts)
- **Voice Memos and Notes:** They show a transcript of the recordings that you make in them, in some languages. [How to transcribe a voice memo.](guide:transcribe-voice-memo-iphone)

For a file that you already have, in a language that the Shortcuts action knows, the action can be all you need. Aiko is for the other languages and for subtitles.
