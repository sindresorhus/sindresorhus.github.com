@QuickAnswer {
	In Finder, press ++Option+Command+W++ to close all its windows. With [Supercharge](/supercharge), ++Command+Q++ closes all Finder windows too, and goes back to the app that you used before. By default, ++Command+Q++ does nothing in Finder.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Finder” section, turn on the setting that makes ++Command+Q++ close all Finder windows and focus the previous app.
1. In Finder, press ++Command+Q++. All Finder windows close, and the app that you used before comes to the front.
}

It also works when you press ++Q++ on Finder in the app switcher (++Command+Tab++).

@Tips {
- Supercharge can also stop ++Command+Q++ from quitting other apps by accident. <guide:prevent-accidental-command-q-mac>
}

## Without an app

In Finder, press ++Option+Command+W++. It closes all windows of the app in front. <source:https://support.apple.com/102650>

++Command+W++ closes only the front window. Finder stays open after you close its windows, as it is always open. Supercharge also brings the app that you used before to the front, so you can go on with it.
