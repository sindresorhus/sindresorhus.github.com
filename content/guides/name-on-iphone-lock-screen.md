@QuickAnswer {
	Write your name and a number to call in [Any Text](/any-text), like “If found, call Ana: +1 555 0100”, and add its widget to the Lock Screen. [Without an app](#without-an-app), the Medical ID of the Health app shows your emergency contacts to anyone who taps “Emergency”, and Lost Mode of Find My shows a message on a lost iPhone.
}

## With Any Text

@Steps {
1. Open [Any Text](/any-text), tap “Widget 1”, and write the text, like “If found, call Ana: +1 555 0100”. Use the number of another person, or your email address, as your own number does not help when the iPhone is lost.
1. Tap the settings button, and turn on “Force Show When Device Is Locked” in “Advanced”.
1. Lock the iPhone, touch and hold the Lock Screen, and tap “Customize”.
1. Tap “Add Widgets” (or the row of widgets, when it already has widgets), choose Any Text, and tap the rectangular widget of “Widget 1”.
1. Tap “Done”.
}

After a restart, iOS shows the text only when the iPhone was unlocked once. This is a limit of iOS for all widgets.

## Without an app

Medical ID shows your name and your emergency contacts on the Lock Screen, but only to someone who knows where to find it:

@Steps {
1. Open the Health app, tap your picture, and tap “Medical ID”.
1. Tap “Get Started” or “Edit”, and add your information. Below “Emergency Contacts”, tap “Add Emergency Contact”.
1. Turn on “Show When Locked”, and tap “Done”.
}

A finder sees it when they swipe up on the Lock Screen, tap “Emergency”, and tap “Medical ID” ([Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph08022b192/ios)).

When the iPhone is lost, open the Find My app on another device, choose the iPhone, tap the More button, and tap “Lost Mode”. You can write a message and a phone number, which the Lock Screen of the lost iPhone then shows ([Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph7cc193cfc/ios)).
