@QuickAnswer {
	macOS has no keyboard shortcut for Terminal. In [Supercharge](/supercharge), set a shortcut for “Terminal” in the “Shortcuts” settings. One press shows Terminal, and the next press hides it. [Without an app](#without-an-app), press ++Command+Space++, type “Terminal”, and press ++Return++.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Shortcuts”.
1. In the “Toggle app” section, click the field next to “Terminal”, and press the keys that you want, like ++Control+Option+T++.
1. Press the shortcut from any app. Terminal opens, with a new window if it has none. Press the shortcut again to hide Terminal and go back to the app that you used before.
}

The “Finder” row above it does the same for Finder. To open Terminal in the folder that you see in Finder, see [how to open Terminal in a folder](guide:open-terminal-in-folder-mac).

## Without an app

[Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/terminal/apd5265185d-f365-44cb-8b09-71a064a42125/mac):

- **Spotlight:** Press ++Command+Space++, type “Terminal”, and press ++Return++.
- **Finder:** Press ++Shift+Command+U++ to open the Utilities folder, and double-click Terminal.
- **Dock:** After you open Terminal, ++Control++-click its icon in the Dock, and choose “Options › Keep in Dock”.

You can also make a keyboard shortcut with the Shortcuts app: [how to open an app with a keyboard shortcut](guide:keyboard-shortcut-to-open-app-mac). Spotlight is fast and needs no setup. Supercharge is for when you go to Terminal and back many times a day.
