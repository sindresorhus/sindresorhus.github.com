@QuickAnswer {
	Turn on “Translate to English” in the settings of [Aiko](../apps/aiko/index.md), and drop the audio or video file on its window. Aiko writes the speech as English text, on your device. Without an app, the Translate app translates the phrases that you speak to it, but not a file.
}

## With Aiko

@Steps {
1. Open [Aiko](../apps/aiko/index.md), and open its settings. On Mac, choose “Aiko › Settings…”.
1. In “Transcription”, turn on “Translate to English”.
1. Drop the audio or video file on the window, or click “Import”. On Mac, you can also choose “File › Import File…”.
1. Wait while Aiko translates. Then copy the text, or click the export button, and choose a format, like “Text” or “SRT (Subtitles)”.
}

The file is never uploaded: the AI model runs on your device. It translates from the language of the audio to English only. To get the text in the original language too, turn off the setting, and transcribe the file again.

@Tips {
- In the Shortcuts app, the “Transcribe Audio” action of Aiko has a “Translate to English” option.
- To make English subtitles for a video, see [how to make subtitles for a video](make-subtitles-for-video-mac.md).
- To translate the result to another language, select the text, Control-click it, and choose “Translate”.
}

## Without an app

The Translate app on iPhone translates what you say, phrase by phrase:

@Steps {
1. Open the Translate app.
1. Tap the languages to choose what to translate from and to.
1. Tap the microphone button, and say a phrase.
}

[Apple: Translate words and phrases on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/translate-text-voice-and-conversations-iphd74cb450f/ios)

This is good for a short phrase or a conversation. For a recording or a video that you already have, use Aiko.
