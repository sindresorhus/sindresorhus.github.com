@QuickAnswer {
	Open [Randa](../apps/randa/index.md) on Apple Watch, set the range to 1 and 6, and tap the number to roll again. Without an app, ask Siri to roll a die.
}

## With Randa

@Steps {
1. Open [Randa](../apps/randa/index.md) on Apple Watch.
1. Scroll down, and set the two numbers to 1 and 6.
1. Tap the big number to roll. Each tap gives a new number, from 1 to 6.
}

On a watch with the double tap gesture, double tap with your fingers to roll without touching the screen. Randa keeps the range while the app is open or in the background, so you only tap the number.

@Tips {
- For a die with more sides, like 20, change the second number.
- For a coin flip, use 0 and 1.
- To never get the same number two times in a row, tap “Settings”, and turn on “Consecutively Unique Numbers”.
}

## Without an app

Ask Siri on the watch: “Roll a die”. Siri shows a number from 1 to 6. For other ranges, ask for a random number, like “Random number between 1 and 20”.

This is free, and fine for a roll now and then. It needs your voice each time, so it is slower for a game where you roll many times, and it does not work well in a loud room.
