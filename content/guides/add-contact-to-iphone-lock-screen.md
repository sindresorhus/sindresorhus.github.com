@QuickAnswer {
	Add the “Contact” widget of [Favorites Widget](/favorites-widget) to the Lock Screen, and choose the person and what a tap does, like “Call” or “Message”. [Without an app](#without-an-app), put a “Shortcut” control in place of the flashlight or the camera button, with a shortcut that calls the person.
}

## With Favorites Widget

@Steps {
1. Open [Favorites Widget](/favorites-widget), tap “Choose Contacts”, and give access to your contacts.
1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, and tap “Customize”.
1. Tap “Add Widgets” (or the widgets that are already there), tap Favorites Widget, and add the round or the wide “Contact” widget.
1. Tap the widget, choose the person in “Contact”, and choose what a tap does in “Action”, like “Call”, “Message”, or “FaceTime Video”.
}

Tap the widget on the Lock Screen to call or message the person. Add one widget for each person.

@Tips {
- Turn off “Show Name” or “Show Photo” in the options of the widget, or give the person another name in “Custom Name”.
- To align the widgets, add the empty “Spacer” widget of Favorites Widget between them.
- iOS asks before each call. To call with one tap, open the app, tap “Quick Call”, and follow the steps there.
- [Add a contact to the Home Screen](guide:add-contact-to-iphone-home-screen) with the same app.
}

## Without an app

The Lock Screen has two controls at the bottom, like the flashlight. You can replace one of them with a shortcut that calls the person:

@Steps {
1. In the Shortcuts app, make a new shortcut, add the “Call” action, and choose the person.
1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, and tap “Customize”.
1. Tap the Remove button on one of the controls at the bottom, and then tap the Add Quick Action button ([Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph4d0e6c351/ios)).
1. Tap “Shortcut”, tap “Choose”, and choose your shortcut, like for a control in Control Center ([Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/shortcuts/apd06a9201d4/ios)).
}

This is free, but the Lock Screen has only two controls, so it is for one or two people.
