@QuickAnswer {
	Open “[System Settings › Menu Bar](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.ControlCenter-Settings.extension)”, and set “Automatically hide and show the menu bar” to “Always”. The menu bar then shows only when you move the pointer to the top of the screen. To turn this on and off with a keyboard shortcut, use [Supercharge](/supercharge).
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Shortcuts”.
1. In the “Toggle feature” section, click the field next to “Auto-hide menu bar”, and press the keys that you want.
1. Press the shortcut to hide the menu bar. Press it again to show it.
}

Hiding the menu bar is useful when a window is below the menu bar, or when you share your screen. Supercharge changes the same setting as System Settings. It turns the hiding on the desktop on or off, and keeps what you chose for apps in full screen. To also have it in the menu of Supercharge, click the button next to “Shown menu items” in the “General” settings, and select “Auto-Hide Menu Bar” below “Toggles”.

## Without an app

[Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlad96d366/mac):

@Steps {
1. Open “[System Settings › Menu Bar](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.ControlCenter-Settings.extension)”.
1. Set “Automatically hide and show the menu bar” to one of these:
	- “Always”: The menu bar hides on the desktop and in full screen.
	- “On Desktop Only”: The menu bar hides when no app is in full screen.
	- “In Full Screen Only”: The menu bar hides only for apps in full screen.
	- “Never”: The menu bar always shows.
}

Move the pointer to the top of the screen to show the hidden menu bar. If you keep one choice, this is all you need. Supercharge is for when you change it often.
