@QuickAnswer {
	Say “Add to Bare”, and then the item, or say “Add to Groceries in Bare” for the list with that name in [Bare](/bare). [Without an app](#without-an-app), say something like “Add artichokes to my groceries list” for a list in the Reminders app.
}

## With Bare

@Steps {
1. Open [Bare](/bare) once, and make your list.
1. Say “Add to Bare” to Siri.
1. When Siri asks “What do you want to add?”, say the item, like “2 milk”. Siri tells you that it added the item.
}

The item also shows on the devices of the people that you share the list with. A common item, like milk, goes in its store section, like “Dairy, Cheese & Eggs”, right away. Another item gets its section the next time you open Bare. “2 milk” adds two of the item, like when you type it in the app.

@Tips {
- Without a list name, the item goes to your first list. To add to another list, say its name, like “Add to Hardware Store in Bare”.
- Say “Open Groceries in Bare” to open the list.
- In the Shortcuts app, the “Add Item” action of Bare adds items from a shortcut. Put each item on its own line to add several.
- [Share the list](guide:share-grocery-list-iphone) with your family, so all of you can add items with Siri.
}

## Without an app

Siri can add an item to a list in the Reminders app. Ask Siri something like “Add artichokes to my groceries list” ([Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph88463e18/ios)). A list of the “Groceries” type sorts the items into sections, like Produce ([Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/105086)).

This is free, and good when you already use Reminders. Reminders is a to-do app, so a grocery list there also has due dates, alerts, and flags.
