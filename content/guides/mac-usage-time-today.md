@QuickAnswer {
	Open the free [Pandan](/pandan) app, and set “Show” to “Total duration today” in its settings. The menu bar then shows how long you used the Mac today. For a report of each app, turn on Screen Time in System Settings.
}

## With Pandan

@Steps {
1. Open [Pandan](/pandan). Its icon is in the menu bar.
1. Open its settings, and in “Show”, choose “Total duration today”, or “Icon and total duration today”.
1. Click the menu bar item to see “Current Session” and “Total Today”.
1. To see more, choose “History…” in the menu. It shows the total of yesterday, the average of the last 7 days and of the weekdays, and your sessions of the last 120 days.
}

Pandan ends a session when you do not use the keyboard or the mouse for some minutes, so the time when you are away from the Mac is not in the total. On a video call, where you only listen and talk, set “End active session” to “only when Mac is locked or sleeping”, so the call counts too.

@Tips {
- The “Get Total Duration Today” action of the Shortcuts app gives the total, for example to log it each evening.
- Pandan can also [remind you to take a break](guide:break-reminder-mac).
- To see how long the Mac has been on since it started, which is not the same as the time of use, see [the uptime guide](guide:uptime-iphone-mac).
}

## Without an app

Screen Time shows how long you used each app ([Apple’s guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlf2c0c770/mac)):

@Steps {
1. Open System Settings, and click “Screen Time” in the sidebar.
1. Click “Manage Screen Time”, and click “Turn On App & Website Activity”.
1. Later, click “Daily Average” to see the usage of each day and each app.
}

Screen Time is free, and it shows more, like the time in each app and on your other devices. To see the total of today without System Settings, add the Screen Time widget to the desktop or to Notification Center. Pandan shows the total in the menu bar, above all windows.
