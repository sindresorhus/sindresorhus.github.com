@QuickAnswer {
	Paste the Spotify link on [song.link](https://song.link), and click Apple Music. To do this by itself each time you copy a music link, turn on “Convert copied music links to Songlink” in [Velja](/velja). The copied link then opens a page with the song in each music service.
}

## With Velja

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Velja](/velja), and click “Extras”.
1. Turn on “Convert copied music links to Songlink”.
1. Copy the link of a song or an album, like in Spotify. Velja changes the link on the clipboard to a Songlink link.
1. Open the link, and click Apple Music, or send it to a friend, who can choose their own music service.
}

It works for the links of songs and albums in Apple Music, Spotify, TIDAL, and Deezer, not for playlists. Velja must be open.

## Without an app

Open [song.link](https://song.link) in your browser, paste the link, and click Apple Music on the page that it shows. This is the same Songlink page, so it is enough for a link now and then. You can also search for the song in the Music app. Velja is faster when you share music links often.
