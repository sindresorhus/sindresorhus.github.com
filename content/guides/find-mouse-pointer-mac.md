@QuickAnswer {
	Move the mouse fast back and forth, or wiggle a finger on the trackpad. macOS makes the pointer big for a moment, so you see it. To move the pointer to the center of the main display, set a keyboard shortcut for “Center mouse pointer on primary display” in [Supercharge](/supercharge).
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open [Supercharge](/supercharge), and give it the access that it asks for in [“Device Control and Data Access”](/apps/faq#device-control-access).
1. Open the Supercharge settings, and click “Shortcuts”.
1. In the “Action” section, click the field next to “Center mouse pointer on primary display”, and press a keyboard shortcut.
1. Press the shortcut. The pointer moves to the center of the main display.
}

To get the pointer at the center each time you come back to the Mac, open “Tweaks”, and turn on “Center mouse pointer on primary display when waking and unlocking” in the “System” section. This helps most with more than one display, where the pointer can be on any of them.

## Without an app

The shake is on by default. If it does not work, turn it on:

@Steps {
1. Open “[System Settings › Accessibility › Display](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Accessibility-Settings.extension)”.
1. Turn on “Shake mouse pointer to locate”. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/unac089/mac>
}

In the same settings, “Pointer size” makes the pointer bigger all the time, and “Pointer outline color” gives it a color that is easy to see. With more than one display, you must still look for the pointer on each one.
