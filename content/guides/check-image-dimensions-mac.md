@QuickAnswer {
	Select the image in Finder, press ++Command+I++, and look at “Dimensions” in the “More Info” section. To see the dimensions in the right-click menu, and copy them with a click, use [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md).
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md), click “Tweaks”, and click “Configure…” next to “Context menu items”.
1. In the “Main Menu” tab, turn on “Image/Video Dimensions”.
1. Right-click an image or a video in Finder. The menu shows its dimensions, like “1920×1080”. Click them to copy them.
}

It shows when you right-click one file. The right-click items cannot show in synced folders, like iCloud Drive and Dropbox.

## Without an app

@Steps {
1. Select the image in Finder, and press ++Command+I++.
1. In the Get Info window, open “More Info”. “Dimensions” shows the width and the height in pixels.
}

You can also choose “View › Show Preview” in Finder, which shows the dimensions of the selected image next to the files. The built-in ways are good for a quick look. Supercharge is faster when you check images often, or need to copy the size.
