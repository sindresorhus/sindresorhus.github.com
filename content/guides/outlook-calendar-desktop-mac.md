@QuickAnswer {
	In the free [Plash](/plash) app, choose “Add Website…”, and enter the address of Outlook Calendar on the web. Sign in once in Browsing Mode. Outlook Calendar is then your desktop wallpaper, behind your windows. [Without an app](#without-an-app), add your Outlook account to the Calendar app, and put a Calendar widget on the desktop.
}

## With Plash

@Steps {
1. Open [Plash](/plash). Its icon is in the menu bar.
1. Click the icon, choose “Add Website…”, and enter the address of your calendar. For a work or school account, use `https://outlook.office.com/calendar/`. For a personal account, use `https://outlook.live.com/calendar/`. Click “Add”.
1. Click the icon, and turn on “Browsing Mode”. Sign in to your Microsoft account. If “Open external links in default browser” is on in the settings of Plash, turn it off while you sign in.
1. Turn off “Browsing Mode”.
}

Outlook Calendar is now on the desktop, behind your windows, in the view that you chose, like the week. Plash keeps you signed in. To add or change an event, turn on “Browsing Mode” again.

@Tips {
- To keep the calendar up to date, turn on “Reload every” in the settings.
- For Google Calendar, see [how to show Google Calendar on the desktop](guide:google-calendar-on-desktop-mac).
}

## Without an app

macOS cannot show a website on the desktop, but it can show your Outlook events in a widget:

@Steps {
1. In the Calendar app, choose “Calendar › Add Account…”, select “Microsoft Exchange”, and sign in with your Outlook account. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/calendar/add-or-delete-calendar-accounts-icl4308d6701/mac>
1. ++Control++-click the wallpaper, and choose “Edit Widgets…”.
1. Search for “Calendar”, and drag a widget to the desktop.
}

The widget is the simple way to see your next events. Plash is for when you want the whole Outlook Calendar page, like the week view, on the desktop itself.
