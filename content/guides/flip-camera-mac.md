@QuickAnswer {
	Open [Camera Preview](/camera-preview), and press ++Shift+Command+H++ (“Camera › Flip Horizontally”) to flip the live image of the camera. [Without an app](#without-an-app), Photo Booth can flip only the photos that you take, with “Edit › Flip Photo”.
}

## With Camera Preview

@Steps {
1. Open [Camera Preview](/camera-preview). It shows the image of your camera in a window.
1. By default, the image is not mirrored, so you see yourself like the others see you. To see yourself like in a mirror, choose “Camera › Flip Horizontally”, or press ++Shift+Command+H++.
1. To turn the image upside down, choose “Camera › Flip Vertically”, or press ++Shift+Command+V++.
}

Each camera keeps its own flip, so a camera that points down at your desk can stay upside down. The photos that you take with ++Return++ have the same flip as the window.

To only check your camera before a call, see [how to see yourself on the camera](guide:see-camera-mac). The flip is only in Camera Preview. It does not change the image that other apps get from the camera, like a video call app.

## Without an app

- **Photo Booth:** Choose “Edit › Flip Photo” to flip a photo that you took. Choose “Edit › Auto Flip New Items” to flip all new photos. The live image always shows as a mirror image.
- **Zoom:** Open the settings of Zoom, click “Video”, and turn off “Mirror my video” to see yourself as the others see you.
- **FaceTime:** FaceTime shows you as a mirror image, and has no setting for it.

In a video call, the others see you as the camera sees you, not as a mirror image, also when your own view is a mirror image. So text that you hold up looks backward to you, but it looks correct to the others.
