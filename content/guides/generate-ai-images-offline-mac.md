@QuickAnswer {
	Download the AI model once in [Imago](../apps/imago/index.md). Then type a description of the image, and press ++Return++. The images are made on your Mac, also without the internet. Image Playground, the built-in app, also uses Apple servers.
}

## With Imago

Imago is free, and needs an Apple silicon Mac and 20 GB of free disk space. It works best with 16 GB of memory or more. On a Mac with less memory, it shows a warning, and making an image can be slow or fail.

@Steps {
1. Open [Imago](../apps/imago/index.md), and click “Download” to get the AI model. It is 16 GB, so this is the only step that needs the internet, and it can take a while.
1. Type a description of the image, like “a red fox in the snow, watercolor”, and press ++Return++.
1. When the image is ready, click it to see it larger, and press ++Space++ to save it.
}

After the download, Imago makes images without the internet. The prompts and the images stay on your Mac.

@Tips {
- Add a reference image to guide the style or the content of the new image.
- Right-click an image, and choose “Generate More” for more images of the same prompt.
- To keep the model on another disk, change “Model location” in the settings.
}

## Without an app

The Image Playground app of macOS makes images from a description, in square, portrait, or landscape sizes. Choose a style, or choose “Any Style” and write the style in the description, like “a realistic image of a stork”. It can also make images of people from your photo library. It needs a Mac with Apple Intelligence. Apple says that Image Playground uses server models with a daily limit, and the ChatGPT styles send your description to ChatGPT. So it is not for images that must be made offline. [How to use Image Playground.](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/create-original-images-with-image-playground-mchld5412d00/mac)

Image Playground is good for quick images, when your Mac is online. Imago is for images that must be made on your Mac, also without the internet.
