@QuickAnswer {
	In the Passwords app, turn on “Show Passwords in Menu Bar”. Then, in the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), click “Shortcuts”, and set a shortcut for “Passwords” in the “Toggle menu bar item window” section. [Without an app](#without-an-app), make a shortcut that opens the Passwords app, and give it a keyboard shortcut.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the Passwords app, choose “Passwords › Settings”, and select “Show Passwords in Menu Bar”. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/passwords/mchldd5eb88d/mac>
1. Open [Supercharge](/supercharge), and give it the access that it asks for in [“Device Control and Data Access”](/apps/faq#device-control-access).
1. Open the Supercharge settings, and click “Shortcuts”.
1. In the “Toggle menu bar item window” section, set a shortcut for “Passwords”.
1. Press the shortcut in any app. The small Passwords window opens below the menu bar, like when you click its icon. Press it again to close it.
}

In the small window, you can search for a password and add one.

@Tips {
- The same section has shortcuts for the Weather and Now Playing menu bar items.
- In the Shortcuts app, the “Toggle Passwords Menu Bar Window” action of Supercharge does the same.
}

## Without an app

Make a shortcut with the “Open App” action and the Passwords app, and give it a keyboard shortcut. <guide:keyboard-shortcut-to-open-app-mac>

This opens the full Passwords app, not the small window of the menu bar.
