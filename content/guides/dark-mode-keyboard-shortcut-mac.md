@QuickAnswer {
	macOS has no keyboard shortcut for Dark Mode. In the “Shortcuts” settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), set a shortcut for “Dark mode”, and press it to switch between the light and the dark appearance. [Without an app](#without-an-app), make a shortcut with the “Set Appearance” action in the Shortcuts app, and give it a keyboard shortcut.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Shortcuts”.
1. In the “Toggle feature” section, click the field next to “Dark mode”, and press the keyboard shortcut that you want.
1. Press the shortcut. The Mac changes to the dark appearance. Press it again to go back to the light appearance.
}

The same section has shortcuts for “Night Shift”, “True Tone”, “Grayscale mode”, and, on a MacBook, “Low power mode”.

## Without an app

- **Shortcuts app:** Make a new shortcut in the Shortcuts app, and add the “Set Appearance” action. In the action, click “Turn”, and choose “Toggle”. Then click the details button in the sidebar, click “Add Keyboard Shortcut”, and press the keys.
- **Control Center:** Click Control Center in the menu bar, click the arrow next to “Display”, and click “Dark Mode”.
- **System Settings:** Open “[System Settings › Appearance](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Appearance-Settings.extension)”, and choose “Light”, “Dark”, or “Auto”. “Auto” changes the appearance with your Night Shift schedule, like from sunset to sunrise.

[Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchl52e1c2d2/mac) “Auto” is enough when you only want the dark appearance at night. The shortcut of the Shortcuts app is free. Supercharge is for when you already use it and do not want to make a shortcut.
