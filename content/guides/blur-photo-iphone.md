@QuickAnswer {
	Make a shortcut with the “Blur Images” action of the free [Actions](/actions) app, choose how much blur, and run it on the photos. It blurs the whole photo. To blur only the background of a Portrait photo, use the “Depth Control” slider in the Photos app.
}

## With Actions

@Steps {
1. Install [Actions](/actions), and open it once.
1. In the Shortcuts app, make a new shortcut, and add the “Select Photos” action. To blur many photos at once, turn on “Select Multiple”.
1. Add the [“Blur Images”](/actions#:~:text=Blur%20Images) action of [Actions](/actions), and drag its slider to choose the amount of blur.
1. Add the “Save to Photos” action.
1. Run the shortcut, and choose the photos. The blurred copies are saved in the Photos app, and the originals do not change.
}

The amount is a part of the size of the photo, so 10% looks the same on a small and a large photo. The copies are PNG files without the date and the location of the original.

To make a blurred wallpaper, use [Blear](/blear). <guide:blur-wallpaper-iphone>

## Without an app

The Photos app has no tool that blurs a whole photo. It can blur the background of a Portrait photo:

@Steps {
1. Open the Portrait photo in the Photos app, and tap the Edit button.
1. Tap “Portrait”, and drag the “Depth Control” slider.
1. Tap “Done”.
}

[Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph310a9a220/ios) On supported models, a photo of a person, a dog, or a cat that you took in Photo mode can also become a portrait. To blur only a part of a photo, like a face, you need another app.
