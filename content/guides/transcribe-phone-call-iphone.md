@QuickAnswer {
	During a call, tap the More button, and tap “Call Recording”. The Notes app saves the recording in the “Call Recordings” folder, with a transcript in some languages and regions. For other languages, share the recording to [Aiko](/aiko), which transcribes about 100 languages on your iPhone.
}

## With Aiko

@Steps {
1. During the call, tap the More button, and tap “Call Recording”. Both of you hear that the call is recorded.
1. After the call, open the Notes app, open the “Call Recordings” folder, tap the note, and tap the recording.
1. Tap the More button, tap “Share Audio”, and choose [Aiko](/aiko) in the row of apps. Aiko opens and transcribes the recording.
1. Keep Aiko open until it is done. Then tap the copy button to copy the text, or the export button to save it.
}

Aiko finds the language itself. If it picks the wrong one, choose it in “Audio language” in the settings. The transcription runs on your iPhone, so the call is never uploaded. Aiko does not show who says what, but the transcript of Notes does.

Call recording is not available in all countries and regions, like the EU. Ask the other person before you record.

## Without an app

@Steps {
1. Record the call like in the first step above.
1. In the Notes app, open the “Call Recordings” folder, tap the note, and tap the recording. The transcript shows who says each line.
1. To copy it, tap the More button, and tap “Copy Transcript”.
}

[Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph57c6590e9/ios) The transcript can take some time to show. If your language has a transcript, it is all you need. Aiko is for the other languages, and for subtitles.
