@QuickAnswer {
	Open [Ask AI](../apps/ask-ai/index.md) on your Apple Watch, and speak or write your question. The answer of ChatGPT shows on the watch, and you can tap it to hear it. On watchOS 27, Siri AI also answers general questions, but only on newer watches and iPhones.
}

## With Ask AI

@Steps {
1. Get [Ask AI](../apps/ask-ai/index.md) from the App Store on your iPhone or on the watch.
1. Open Ask AI on the watch, and tap “Ask Anything”. After the first launch, the text input opens at once.
1. Dictate your question, or write it.
1. Read the answer. Tap it to hear it.
1. To ask more, tap the message button at the bottom.
}

The answers are short, to fit the screen of the watch. You can ask in many languages.

@Tips {
- Turn on “Auto-speak answer” in the settings of the app to hear each answer without a tap.
- To open Ask AI with the Action button of an Apple Watch Ultra, add the [“Open Ask AI” shortcut](../apps/ask-ai/index.md#can-i-launch-it-with-the-action-button), and choose it in the settings of the Action button.
- Ask AI is a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no ChatGPT account.
}

## Without an app

On watchOS 27, [Siri AI](https://support.apple.com/127893) answers general questions, like ChatGPT. If it works for you, use it. It needs:

- An Apple Watch Series 9 or later, Ultra 2 or later, or SE 3.
- An iPhone with Apple Intelligence, paired with the watch, with Siri AI turned on.
- English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, or Spanish as the language of the device and of Siri.
- An Apple Account outside the EU and mainland China.
