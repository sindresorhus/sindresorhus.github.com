@QuickAnswer {
	macOS saves a screenshot to a file, or, when you add ++Control++ to the shortcut, like ++Control+Shift+Command+4++, copies it to the clipboard. It cannot do both. [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md) copies each saved screenshot to the clipboard, so you can also paste it.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md), and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Screenshots” section, turn on “Copy screenshots to clipboard”.
1. Take a screenshot as usual, like with ++Shift+Command+4++. It is saved, and you can paste it.
}

It only works with the screenshot tool of macOS, and when it saves screenshots to a folder. If the floating thumbnail is on, the screenshot is copied when the thumbnail goes away.

## Without an app

- **Copy only:** Add ++Control++ to the shortcut, like ++Control+Shift+Command+4++. The screenshot is not saved.
- **Use the thumbnail:** After a screenshot, drag the thumbnail in the corner of the screen into a document or a message.

[Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/take-screenshots-mh26782/mac) These are enough when you only sometimes need both. Supercharge is for when you always want to paste the screenshot that you just took.
