@QuickAnswer {
	In the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), click “Shortcuts”, and set a shortcut for “Finder” in the “Toggle app” section. [Without an app](#without-an-app), press ++Option+Command+Space++ to open a Finder search window.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Shortcuts”.
1. In the “Toggle app” section, click the field next to “Finder”, and press the keyboard shortcut that you want, like ++Control+Option+F++.
1. Press the shortcut in any app. Finder comes to the front, and opens a window if it has none.
1. Press it again to hide Finder.
}

So one shortcut shows and hides Finder, like ++Win+E++ opens File Explorer on Windows. To use shortcuts with only one extra key, like ++Caps Lock+F++, [make Caps Lock a Hyper key](guide:hyper-key-mac).

## Without an app

macOS has no shortcut that shows and hides Finder, but it has these:

- ++Option+Command+Space++ opens a Finder window to search your Mac. You can change it in “[System Settings › Keyboard](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Keyboard-Settings.extension)”: click “Keyboard Shortcuts…”, click “Spotlight”, and change “Show Finder search window”.
- Click the Finder icon at the left end of the Dock.
- When Finder is the active app, ++Command+N++ opens a new window, and ++Shift+Command+H++ opens your home folder.

You can also [give any app a keyboard shortcut](guide:keyboard-shortcut-to-open-app-mac) with the Shortcuts app. That opens Finder, but a second press does not hide it.
