@QuickAnswer {
	Click [Supercharge](/supercharge) in the menu bar, and choose “Desktop Widgets”. Choose it again to show the widgets. [Without an app](#without-an-app), open “[System Settings › Desktop & Dock](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Desktop-Settings.extension)”, and turn off “On Desktop” next to “Show Widgets”.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open [Supercharge](/supercharge), and give it the access that it asks for in [“Device Control and Data Access”](/apps/faq#device-control-access).
1. Click its icon in the menu bar, and choose “Desktop Widgets”. The widgets go away.
1. To show them again, choose “Desktop Widgets” again.
}

To do it with a keyboard shortcut, open the Supercharge settings, click “Shortcuts”, and set a shortcut for “Desktop widgets”. “Desktop Icons & Widgets” hides the [files on the desktop](guide:hide-desktop-icons-mac) too, like before a screen share.

Like the setting of macOS, the hidden widgets show again in Mission Control and when you click the wallpaper to show the desktop.

## Without an app

@Steps {
1. Open “[System Settings › Desktop & Dock](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Desktop-Settings.extension)”.
1. In the “Widgets” section, next to “Show Widgets”, turn off “On Desktop”.
}

To show them again, turn it on. The widgets stay where they were. To remove one widget, right-click it, and choose “Remove Widget”.

The setting is good when you never want the widgets on the desktop. Supercharge is faster when you hide them only for a while.
