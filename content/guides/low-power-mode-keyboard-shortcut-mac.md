@QuickAnswer {
	macOS has no keyboard shortcut for Low Power Mode. On a Mac with a battery, set a shortcut for “Low power mode” in the “Shortcuts” settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and press it to turn Low Power Mode on and off. macOS asks for your password almost each time. [Without an app](#without-an-app), make a shortcut with the “Set Low Power Mode” action in the Shortcuts app, and give it a keyboard shortcut.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Shortcuts”.
1. In the “Toggle feature” section, click the field next to “Low power mode”, and press the keyboard shortcut that you want.
1. Press the shortcut, and enter your administrator password. Press it again to turn Low Power Mode off.
}

Supercharge changes the energy mode for both “On battery” and “On power adapter”. This needs administrator access, so macOS asks for your password almost each time. The field shows only on a Mac with a battery. The menu of Supercharge also has a “Low Power Mode” item, which shows if it is on.

## Without an app

**Shortcuts app**

@Steps {
1. In the Shortcuts app, click the add button to make a new shortcut, and add the “Set Low Power Mode” action.
1. In the action, click “Turn”, and choose “Toggle”.
1. Click the details button in the sidebar, click “Add Keyboard Shortcut”, and press the keys.
}

**System Settings**

Open “[System Settings › Battery](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Battery-Settings.extension)” (“Energy” on a Mac without a battery), and choose “Low Power Mode” for “On battery” or “On power adapter” in “Energy Mode”. You can also choose the energy mode in Control Center. <source:https://support.apple.com/101613>

The shortcut of the Shortcuts app is free. Supercharge is for when you already use it and do not want to make a shortcut.
