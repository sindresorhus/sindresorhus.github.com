@QuickAnswer {
	Open the free [Pandan](/pandan) app. It shows in the menu bar how long you have used your Mac without a break, and it can remind you at the interval you choose. [Without an app](#without-an-app), set a timer in the Clock app, as macOS has no break reminder.
}

## With Pandan

@Steps {
1. Open [Pandan](/pandan). The time that you have used the Mac shows in the menu bar.
1. Open its settings, click “Notifications”, and choose an interval in “Show a gentle reminder”, like “every 25 minutes”.
1. In “Type”, choose how the reminder shows: a popover, a notification, or a HUD in the middle of all screens.
}

Pandan knows that you are away when you do not use the keyboard or the mouse, and it starts a new session when you come back, so a break resets the time. Set how long you must be away in “End session when idle for” in the “General” settings. On a video call, you only listen and talk, so the session can end. To prevent this, make the time longer, or set “End active session” to “only when Mac is locked or sleeping”.

The reminder can also play a sound, or run a shortcut from the Shortcuts app, like one that plays your own sound. The “History” window shows your sessions of the last 120 days. For a short eye break every 20 minutes, see [how to follow the 20-20-20 rule](guide:20-20-20-rule-mac).

## Without an app

macOS has no reminder to take a break. Screen Time shows how long you used the Mac each day, but it does not remind you to take breaks. To get a reminder:

@Steps {
1. Open the Clock app, and choose “View › Timers”.
1. Set the time until your next break, like 25 minutes, and click “Start”.
}

A notification appears when the timer ends. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/clock-mac/apdw3d5aebf9/mac>

But the timer does not know if you were away from the Mac, so you must start it again after each break.
