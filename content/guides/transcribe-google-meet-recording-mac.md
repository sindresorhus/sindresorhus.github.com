@QuickAnswer {
	Download the recording from the “Google Meet” folder in Google Drive, and drop it on the window of [Aiko](/aiko). Aiko writes the text on your Mac, also when nobody turned on the transcript in the meeting, or when the meeting was in a language that Meet does not transcribe.
}

## With Aiko

@Steps {
1. In Google Drive, open the “Google Meet” folder, and open the folder of the meeting. Select the recording, click More, and click Download.
1. Open [Aiko](/aiko), and drop the file on its window, or click “Import”.
1. Wait while Aiko transcribes. A long meeting takes some minutes.
1. Click the export button in the toolbar, and choose “Text”, “Text with Timestamps”, or “SRT (Subtitles)”.
}

Aiko uses the Whisper model of OpenAI on your Mac, so the meeting is never uploaded. It finds the language itself. [More about transcribing audio.](guide:transcribe-audio-to-text-mac) Try Aiko free for 14 days with [the trial](/aiko#trial).

@Tips {
- The recording is in the Google Drive of the meeting organizer. The other people get a shortcut to it in their Drive. <source:https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308681>
- For a meeting that Meet did not record, [record the audio of the call](guide:record-call-audio-mac) with Recordia, and then choose “Previous Recording › Transcribe with Aiko” in its menu.
}

## Without an app

Meet makes a transcript when Transcripts is on during the meeting, on some Google Workspace editions. The transcript is saved in the same “Google Meet” folder. Transcripts are available in English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish. <source:https://support.google.com/meet/answer/12849897>

Some plans that can record a meeting, like Google One with 2 TB or more, are not in the list of editions with Transcripts. [Who can record a meeting.](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308681)
