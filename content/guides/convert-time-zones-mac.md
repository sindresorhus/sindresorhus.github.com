@QuickAnswer {
	In [Zone Bar](../apps/zone-bar/index.md), right-click the time zone of the time that you have, choose “Go to Date & Time…”, and set the day and the time. All your clocks then show that moment in their time zone. Without an app, the World Clock of the Clock app shows the time now in each city.
}

## With Zone Bar

@Steps {
1. Add your time zones to [Zone Bar](../apps/zone-bar/index.md). [How to add them.](show-time-zones-menu-bar-mac.md)
1. Click a clock in the menu bar. Right-click the time zone of the time that you have, like New York for a meeting at 15:00 New York time, and choose “Go to Date & Time…”.
1. Choose the day in the calendar, and type the time in the time field. The time is in the time zone that you right-clicked.
1. Read the time in your other clocks.
1. Press ++Esc++, or click the reset button below the calendar, to go back to the time now.
}

To send the time to someone, right-click a clock while it shows the time, and choose “Date and Time” or “Full Info” below “Copy”. “Full Info” also has the name of the time zone.

@Tips {
- For a quick look, like “what time is it there in 3 hours”, drag the time travel slider instead.
- The conversion follows daylight saving time on the day that you choose.
}

## Without an app

The Clock app of macOS shows the time now in each city:

@Steps {
1. Open the Clock app, and click “World Clock”.
1. Add the cities, and compare their times with yours.
}

This is enough to see the difference between two cities now. To find the time of a meeting on another day, like after a change to daylight saving time, you must count the hours yourself.
