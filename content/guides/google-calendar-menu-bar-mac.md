@QuickAnswer {
	In the settings of [Menu Bar Calendar](../apps/menu-bar-calendar/index.md), turn on “Use Google Calendar instead of the Calendar app for viewing dates and creating events”. Then click a date in the calendar in the menu bar, and it opens in Google Calendar in your browser. Without an app, add your Google account to the Calendar app, and see your events in its widgets.
}

## With Menu Bar Calendar

@Steps {
1. Open [Menu Bar Calendar](../apps/menu-bar-calendar/index.md), and click its icon in the menu bar.
1. Click the more button in the calendar, and choose “Settings…”.
1. Turn on “Use Google Calendar instead of the Calendar app for viewing dates and creating events”.
1. Click the icon, and click a date. Google Calendar opens at that day.
}

To make an event in Google Calendar, right-click a day, and choose “New Event”. With “Show week numbers” on, click a week number to open that week. To make an event with a click, set “Clicking calendar day” to “Creates new event”.

Menu Bar Calendar does not show your events, only the month. [How to show a calendar in the menu bar.](calendar-menu-bar-mac.md) To keep Google Calendar open on the desktop, see [how to put Google Calendar on the desktop](google-calendar-on-desktop-mac.md).

## Without an app

macOS cannot open Google Calendar from the menu bar, but the Calendar app can show your Google events:

@Steps {
1. In the Calendar app, choose “Calendar › Add Account”.
1. Select Google, click “Continue”, and sign in.
}

[Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/guide/calendar/icl4308d6701/mac) Your Google events are then in the Calendar app and in its widgets. To see a widget from the menu bar, click the date and time, and add a Calendar widget with “Edit Widgets”.
