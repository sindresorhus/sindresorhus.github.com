@QuickAnswer {
	In the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), click “Tweaks”, and turn on “Mute microphone while typing”. Then the microphone is muted while you type during a call, and unmuted shortly after you stop. [Without an app](#without-an-app), FaceTime has “Voice Isolation”, which blocks the sounds around you.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open [Supercharge](/supercharge), and give it the access that it asks for in [“Device Control and Data Access”](/apps/faq#device-control-access).
1. Open the Supercharge settings, and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “System” section, turn on “Mute microphone while typing”.
}

It works only while an app uses the microphone, like a call app, so it does nothing at other times. It mutes the microphone of the Mac, so it works in all call apps.

@Tips {
- To mute and unmute the microphone yourself, set a keyboard shortcut for it. <guide:mute-microphone-shortcut-mac>
}

## Without an app

On a FaceTime call, “Voice Isolation” makes your voice clear and blocks the sounds around you:

@Steps {
1. During a FaceTime video call, click the Video button in the menu bar.
1. Click the arrow next to “Mic Mode”, and select “Voice Isolation”. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/facetime/fctme7c07113/mac>
}

On an audio call, click the Audio button in the menu bar, and select “Voice Isolation”. Most call apps also have a mute button and a shortcut. Press it before you type, and again when you stop.
