@QuickAnswer {
	In the settings of [QuickGPT](/quickgpt), click the field of “Toggle window”, and press a keyboard shortcut. Then press the shortcut in any app to show or hide ChatGPT. [Without an app](#without-an-app), make a web app of ChatGPT with “File › Add to Dock” in Safari, and give it a keyboard shortcut.
}

## With QuickGPT

@Steps {
1. Open [QuickGPT](/quickgpt), and sign in to ChatGPT in its window.
1. Choose “QuickGPT › Settings…”.
1. Click the field of “Toggle window”, and press the keyboard shortcut that you want, like ++Control+Option+G++.
1. Press the shortcut in any app to show ChatGPT, and press it again to hide it.
}

@Tips {
- To show ChatGPT below an icon in the menu bar, choose “Menu Bar” in “Show in” in the settings. The app starts again. The window then hides when you click outside it. To keep it open, turn on “Pin window”.
- The shortcut works only while the app is running. Turn on “Launch at login” in the settings.
- [Why use this app when ChatGPT has an app for Mac?](/quickgpt#why-this-when-chatgpt-has-an-official-macos-app)
}

## Without an app

Safari can save ChatGPT as a web app ([Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/104996)):

@Steps {
1. In Safari, open chatgpt.com.
1. Choose “File › Add to Dock”, and click “Add”.
1. Open the web app from the Dock, and sign in to ChatGPT. The web app does not share the sign-in of Safari.
}

The web app is in the Applications folder of your home folder, so you can [give it a keyboard shortcut](guide:keyboard-shortcut-to-open-app-mac) like any app. The shortcut opens the app, but it does not hide it again. QuickGPT shows and hides ChatGPT with the same shortcut, and can show it from the menu bar.
