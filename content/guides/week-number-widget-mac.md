@QuickAnswer {
	Open the free [Week Number](/week-number) app once. Then ++Control++-click the wallpaper, choose “Edit Widgets…”, search for “Week Number”, and add its widget. macOS has no widget for the week number. Only the Calendar app can show week numbers.
}

## With Week Number

@Steps {
1. Open [Week Number](/week-number). It shows the week number in the menu bar.
1. ++Control++-click the wallpaper, and choose “Edit Widgets…”.
1. Search for “Week Number”, and click the size that you want, small or medium.
1. Drag the widget to where you want it on the desktop, and click “Done”.
}

The widget shows the number in large text, with “Week” below it. You can change the word below the number in the settings of the widget, like to the word in your language. The widget changes to the new number at the start of each week.

The number follows the region settings of your Mac, like the Calendar app does. To also see the week number in the menu bar, see [how to show the week number in the menu bar](guide:show-week-number-menu-bar-mac).

## Without an app

macOS has no widget for the week number, but the Calendar app can show the week numbers:

@Steps {
1. Open the Calendar app, and choose “Calendar › Settings”.
1. Click “Advanced”, and turn on “Show week numbers”.
}

The numbers then show in the month and the week views of the Calendar app. This is enough when you look at your calendar often. A widget shows the number without opening an app.
