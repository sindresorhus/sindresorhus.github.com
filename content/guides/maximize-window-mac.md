@QuickAnswer {
	Press ++Fn+Control+F++, or hold the pointer over the green button of the window, and click “Fill”. The window fills the desktop, and does not go fullscreen. To make a click on the green button do this, use [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md).
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md), and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Apps & Windows” section, turn on “Fill window when clicking green traffic light button”.
1. Click the green button of a window. The window fills the screen. Click it again to get the previous size back.
}

Hold ++Option++ while you click the green button to go fullscreen. The tweak uses the Fill action of macOS, so it needs “Displays have separate Spaces” on in “[System Settings › Desktop & Dock](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Desktop-Settings.extension)”.

## Without an app

- **Keyboard:** Press ++Fn+Control+F++.
- **Green button:** Hold the pointer over the green button, and click “Fill” below “Fill & Arrange”. To get the previous size back, ++Option++-click the green button.
- **Title bar:** In “[System Settings › Desktop & Dock](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Desktop-Settings.extension)”, set “Double-click a window’s title bar to” to “Fill”. Then double-click the title bar of a window.

[Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/work-with-app-windows-mchlp2469/mac) These are enough for most people. Supercharge is for when you click the green button by habit and do not want fullscreen.
