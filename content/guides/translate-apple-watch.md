@QuickAnswer {
	Open the Translate app on your Apple Watch, tap the language to translate to, tap the microphone button, and say the phrase. For a language that Translate does not have, or to ask what a phrase means, ask [Ask AI](/ask-ai).
}

## With Ask AI

@Steps {
1. Open [Ask AI](/ask-ai) on your Apple Watch, and tap “Ask Anything”.
1. Say or write what to translate, and to which language, like “Translate ‘Two coffees, please’ to Greek”.
1. Read the translation. Tap it to hear it.
}

You can also ask what a translation app cannot answer, like how to say something politely, or what a dish on a menu is. The [app page](/ask-ai) lists the languages that you can ask in. Ask AI needs the internet.

## Without an app

watchOS 11 and later have the Translate app:

@Steps {
1. Open the Translate app on your Apple Watch.
1. Tap the language to translate to.
1. Tap the microphone button, and say the phrase.
1. Tap the play button to hear the translation.
}

[Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apd25ec90584/watchos) To translate without the internet, tap the More button, tap “Downloaded Languages”, and download both languages. The Translate app is free and works offline, so use it when it has your languages.
