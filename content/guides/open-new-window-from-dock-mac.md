@QuickAnswer {
	On Mac, press ++Command+N++ in the app to open a new window. To open one from the Dock, like with a ++Shift++-click on the taskbar of Windows, turn on “Open new window by Shift+clicking running app in Dock” in [Supercharge](/supercharge), and ++Shift++-click the icon.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open [Supercharge](/supercharge), and give it the access that it asks for in [“Device Control and Data Access”](/apps/faq#device-control-access).
1. Open the Supercharge settings, and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Dock” section, turn on “Open new window by Shift+clicking running app in Dock”.
1. ++Shift++-click the icon of an open app in the Dock. The app comes to the front with a new window.
}

Supercharge presses ++Command+N++ in the app for you. So in some apps, you get a new tab, or nothing happens. When the app is not open, a click opens it as usual.

## Without an app

- **Keyboard:** Click the app in the Dock, and press ++Command+N++. In Finder, this is a new Finder window.
- **Dock menu:** ++Control++-click the icon of the app in the Dock. Some apps have a new window item there, like “New Window” in Safari and “New Finder Window” in Finder.
- **Menu bar:** Choose “File › New Window” in the app. The name can be different, like “New Document”.

Most Mac apps keep running with all their windows, so a click on the icon only brings them to the front. ++Command+N++ is the way of the Mac.
