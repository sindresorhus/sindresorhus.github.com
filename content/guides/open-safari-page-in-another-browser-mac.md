@QuickAnswer {
	Install the free [Shareful](/shareful), click the share button in Safari, choose “Open In…”, and click the browser. [Without an app](#without-an-app), turn on the features for web developers in Safari, and choose “Develop › Open Page With”.
}

## With Shareful

@Steps {
1. Install [Shareful](/shareful), and open it one time.
1. In Safari, click the share button, and click “Edit Extensions…” at the bottom of the menu. Turn on “Open In…”, and click “Done”. You do this only one time.
1. Click the share button, and choose “Open In…”.
1. Click the browser, like Chrome or Firefox. The page opens in it.
}

“Open In…” works in all apps with a share button, so you can also open a photo from Photos or a note from Notes in another app. [How to add Copy and Save As to the share menu.](guide:add-copy-save-to-share-menu-mac)

If you cannot find “Open In…”, restart the Mac. [More help.](/shareful#the-share-services-do-not-show-up-in-system-settings)

## Without an app

Safari has this in its Develop menu, which is hidden at first:

@Steps {
1. Choose “Safari › Settings”, click “Advanced”, and turn on “Show features for web developers”.
1. Open the page, and choose “Develop › Open Page With”.
1. Choose the browser.
}

The Develop menu lists the other browsers on your Mac ([Apple’s documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/safari-developer-tools/develop-menu)). This works well if you only need it in Safari. You can also copy the address with ++Command+L++ and ++Command+C++, and paste it in the other browser.
