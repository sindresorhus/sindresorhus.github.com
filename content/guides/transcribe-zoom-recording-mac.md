@QuickAnswer {
	Drop the Zoom recording on the window of [Aiko](../apps/aiko/index.md). It writes the text on your Mac, also for a meeting in another language, and exports it as text or subtitles. Zoom itself makes a transcript only of a recording in its cloud, which needs a paid plan.
}

## With Aiko

@Steps {
1. In Finder, open the “Zoom” folder in your Documents folder, and open the folder of the meeting. If you changed where Zoom saves recordings, the “Recording” settings of Zoom show the folder.
1. Open [Aiko](../apps/aiko/index.md), and drop the MP4 file of the meeting on its window, or click “Import”.
1. Wait while Aiko transcribes. A long meeting takes some minutes.
1. Click the export button in the toolbar, and choose “Text”, “Text with Timestamps”, or “SRT (Subtitles)”.
}

Aiko uses the Whisper model of OpenAI on your Mac, so the meeting is never uploaded. It finds the language itself. [More about transcribing audio.](transcribe-audio-to-text-mac.md) Try Aiko free for 14 days with [the trial](../apps/aiko/index.md#trial).

## Without an app

Zoom makes an audio transcript of a cloud recording, on the Pro, Business, Education, and Enterprise plans. It does not make one for a recording that is saved on your computer. [Zoom’s instructions.](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0065911)

On the Mac, Voice Memos and Notes show a transcript of the recordings that you make in them. [How to transcribe audio to text.](transcribe-audio-to-text-mac.md)
