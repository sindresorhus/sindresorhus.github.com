@QuickAnswer {
	Press ++Option+Command+Esc++, select the app in the Force Quit window, and click “Force Quit”. With [Supercharge](/supercharge), hold ++Option++ and middle-click the icon of the app in the Dock to force quit it at once.
}

Quit an app normally first, with ++Command+Q++, so it can save your changes. Force quit it only when it does not respond.

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open [Supercharge](/supercharge), and give it the access that it asks for in [“Device Control and Data Access”](/apps/faq#device-control-access).
1. Open the Supercharge settings, and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Dock” section, set “When middle-clicking an app in the Dock” to “Quit App”.
1. Hold ++Option++, and middle-click the icon of the app in the Dock. The app quits at once.
}

A middle-click without ++Option++ quits the app normally.

@Tips {
- Without a mouse with a middle button, set a trackpad gesture as a middle-click. <guide:middle-click-trackpad-mac>
}

## Without an app

@Steps {
1. Press ++Option+Command+Esc++, or choose “Apple menu › Force Quit…”.
1. Select the app in the Force Quit window, and click “Force Quit”. <source:https://support.apple.com/102586>
}

Finder is always open, so for Finder the button is “Relaunch”.
