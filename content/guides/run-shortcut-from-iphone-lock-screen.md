@QuickAnswer {
	For one or two shortcuts, replace the flashlight or the camera button at the bottom of the Lock Screen with the “Shortcut” control. For more shortcuts, or to keep the flashlight and the camera, [Quick Launch](/quick-launch) adds up to four buttons below the clock, and each can run a shortcut.
}

## With Quick Launch

@Steps {
1. Open [Quick Launch](/quick-launch), and tap the add button.
1. Choose “Shortcut” as the action. In “Shortcut Name”, write the name of the shortcut exactly as it shows in the Shortcuts app.
1. In “Icon”, tap “Choose”, and choose the icon of the button, like “Symbol” or “Emoji”. Then tap the checkmark button to save the widget.
1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, and tap “Customize”.
1. Tap the widget area below the clock, choose “Quick Launch”, and add the “Open Stuff” widget. Tap the widget, and choose the widget that you made.
}

Tap the button on the Lock Screen to run the shortcut. iOS does not let apps run a shortcut in the background, so the Shortcuts app opens and runs it. To go back to the Home Screen when the shortcut is done, add the “Go to Home Screen” action at the end of the shortcut.

The same widget can also [open an app](guide:add-app-to-iphone-lock-screen) or [a website](guide:add-website-to-iphone-lock-screen).

## Without an app

The two buttons at the bottom of the Lock Screen, the flashlight and the camera, can be other controls, like one that runs a shortcut:

@Steps {
1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, and tap “Customize”.
1. Tap the remove button on the flashlight or the camera control, and then tap the add button in its place.
1. Choose the “Shortcut” control of the Shortcuts app, and choose your shortcut.
}

[Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph4d0e6c351/ios) If two buttons are enough for you, use them. Each control that you change replaces the flashlight or the camera. Quick Launch uses the widget area below the clock, so you can have up to four buttons in addition to the two controls.
