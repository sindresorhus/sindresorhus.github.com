@QuickAnswer {
	Add the “Contact” widget of [Favorites Widget](/favorites-widget) to the Home Screen, choose the person, and set “Action” to “Open in WhatsApp”. A tap on the widget opens the chat. [Without an app](#without-an-app), open a WhatsApp link with the phone number in Safari, and add the page to the Home Screen.
}

## With Favorites Widget

@Steps {
1. Open [Favorites Widget](/favorites-widget), tap “Choose Contacts”, and give access to your contacts.
1. Add a “Contact” widget of Favorites Widget to the Home Screen. [How to add a contact widget.](guide:add-contact-to-iphone-home-screen)
1. In the options of the widget, tap “Contact”, and choose the phone number of the person.
1. Tap “Action”, and choose “Open in WhatsApp”.
}

The widget shows the photo and the name of the person. To change the options later, touch and hold the widget, and tap “Edit Widget”.

@Tips {
- The phone number must have its country code, like +1 or +47. WhatsApp needs the international number, and Favorites Widget does not add a country code, so add it to the number in the Contacts app.
- The action needs a phone number, not an email address.
- “Open in Telegram” opens a chat in Telegram in the same way.
}

## Without an app

WhatsApp has links that open a chat with a phone number ([WhatsApp’s instructions](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934)). Safari can add a link to the Home Screen ([Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph42ab2f3a7/ios)):

@Steps {
1. In Safari, open `https://wa.me/` with the full phone number in international format after it, like `https://wa.me/15551234567`. Leave out the plus, zeros at the start, brackets, and dashes.
1. Tap the Page Menu button, and then “Share”. With the Bottom or Top tab layout, tap the Share button.
1. Tap “Add to Home Screen”, and then “Add”.
}

This is free. The icon is the icon of the website, not the photo of the person, so many chats look the same on the Home Screen. A shortcut in the Shortcuts app that opens the same link can have a photo as its icon, but it takes more steps.
