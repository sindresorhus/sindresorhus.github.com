@QuickAnswer {
	In the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), click “Tools”, click “Choose…” below “Hidden accent colors”, and pick a color of the iMac or the MacBook Neo. Then quit and open your apps again. [Without an app](#without-an-app), you can choose only the normal accent colors in “[System Settings › Appearance](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Appearance-Settings.extension)”.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Tools”.
1. Below “Hidden accent colors”, click “Choose…”.
1. Click a color in “iMac 2021 Accents” or “MacBook Neo Accents”. The preview shows the color.
1. Quit and open your apps again to see the new color.
}

To go back to your earlier color, click “Reset” in the same window.

On an iMac or a MacBook Neo, the color of the model is built in, and Supercharge cannot change it or bring it back.

## Without an app

In “[System Settings › Appearance](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Appearance-Settings.extension)”, choose an accent color. It changes the buttons, menus, and other controls. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchl52e1c2d2/mac>

The colors of the iMac and the MacBook Neo are not in this list on other Macs.
