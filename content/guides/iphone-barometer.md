@QuickAnswer {
	The iPhone has a barometer, but no built-in app shows what it measures. The “Get Barometric Pressure” action of the free [Actions](../apps/actions/index.md) app reads the sensor in a shortcut, and gives the air pressure.
}

## With Actions

@Steps {
1. Install [Actions](../apps/actions/index.md), and open it once.
1. In the Shortcuts app, make a new shortcut, and add the “Get Barometric Pressure” action.
1. Add the “Show Content” action.
1. Run the shortcut. The first time, allow access to Motion & Fitness.
}

The normal air pressure at sea level is about 1013 hPa (millibars), which is 101.3 kPa. Add the shortcut to the Home Screen to read the pressure with one tap.

@Tips {
- The “Get Elevation” action of Actions also uses the barometer, and gives the height above sea level.
- To follow the weather, run the shortcut at the same place a few times a day. Falling pressure often means that bad weather comes.
}

The action is not on the Mac.

## Without an app

The Weather app shows the air pressure of your place from a weather service, not from the sensor of your iPhone. For the weather, this is often enough. Use Actions when you want the reading of the sensor itself, like to see how the pressure changes when you go up a mountain.
