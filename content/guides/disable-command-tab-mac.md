@QuickAnswer {
	In the “Tweaks” settings of [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md), turn on “Disable ++Command+Tab++ app switcher”. Without an app, “System Settings › Keyboard › Keyboard Shortcuts” turns off the keyboard shortcuts of macOS that are in its lists.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md), and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Apps & Windows” section, turn on “Disable ++Command+Tab++ app switcher”.
1. Press ++Command+Tab++. The app switcher does not open.
}

To get the app switcher back, turn the setting off. The setting is for when you do not use the app switcher and open it by accident.

## Without an app

macOS lets you turn off some of its own keyboard shortcuts:

@Steps {
1. Open “[System Settings › Keyboard](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Keyboard-Settings.extension)”, and click “Keyboard Shortcuts”.
1. Select a category in the sidebar, like “Mission Control” or “Spotlight”.
1. Deselect the checkbox next to the shortcut.
}

[Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp2262/mac) If the shortcut that you press by accident is in one of these lists, turn it off there. For the app switcher of ++Command+Tab++, use the setting of Supercharge.
