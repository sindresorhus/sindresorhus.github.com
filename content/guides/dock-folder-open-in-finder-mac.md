@QuickAnswer {
	Hold ++Option+Command++ while you click a folder in the Dock, like Downloads, and it opens in Finder. To open it in Finder with a plain click, turn on “Clicking a Dock folder reveals it in Finder instead of showing its contents” in [Supercharge](/supercharge).
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open [Supercharge](/supercharge), and give it the access that it asks for in [“Device Control and Data Access”](/apps/faq#device-control-access).
1. Open the Supercharge settings, and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Dock” section, turn on “Clicking a Dock folder reveals it in Finder instead of showing its contents”.
1. Click a folder in the Dock. A Finder window opens with the folder.
}

To see the stack of files again, right-click the folder. To get the menu of the folder, like “Sort by”, hold ++Option++ while you right-click it. Smart folders still show their stack.

## Without an app

- **Open the folder:** Hold ++Option+Command++ while you click the folder in the Dock. Supercharge does the same for you.
- **From the stack:** Click the folder, and click “Open in Finder” in the stack.

To change how the stack looks, ++Control++-click the folder, and choose an item below “Display as” or “View content as”.
