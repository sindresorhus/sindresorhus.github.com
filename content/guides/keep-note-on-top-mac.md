@QuickAnswer {
	Click [Scratchpad](/scratchpad) in the menu bar. Its note stays in front of other windows, and stays open while you use other apps. [Without an app](#without-an-app), open a note in Stickies, and choose “Window › Float on Top”.
}

## With Scratchpad

@Steps {
1. Open [Scratchpad](/scratchpad), and click its icon in the menu bar.
1. Type your note, like the steps that you follow or a number that you copy from.
1. Click another app. The note stays open, in front of the other windows.
1. To close the note, click the icon again.
}

“Pin Window” in the “…” menu of the note is on at first, so the note does not close when you click another app. Turn it off to close the note each time you click outside it, like a menu.

@Tips {
- If you show Scratchpad in the Dock, not in the menu bar, choose “Window › Stay on Top”, or press ++Control+Command+T++.
- Turn on “Show on all spaces” in the settings, so the note stays when you go to another desktop.
- Set a keyboard shortcut in the settings to show and hide the note from any app.
}

Scratchpad has one note in plain text. [How to take quick notes on Mac.](guide:quick-notes-mac)

## Without an app

The Stickies app of macOS can keep a note in front:

@Steps {
1. Open Stickies, and click the note, or choose “File › New Note”.
1. Choose “Window › Float on Top”. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/stickies/welcome/mac>
}

Each note has its own setting, so you can keep one note in front and the others behind. This works well and is free. Scratchpad opens from the menu bar or a keyboard shortcut, and syncs the note with your iPhone.
