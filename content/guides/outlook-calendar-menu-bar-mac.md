@QuickAnswer {
	Add your Outlook account to the Calendar app with “Calendar › Add Account…”. Then click [Dato](/dato) in the menu bar to see a calendar and your Outlook events. macOS has no calendar in the menu bar. It shows Outlook events only in the Calendar app and its widgets.
}

## With Dato

@Steps {
1. In the Calendar app, choose “Calendar › Add Account…”, select “Microsoft Exchange”, and sign in with your Outlook or Microsoft 365 account. Keep “Calendars” on. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/calendar/add-or-delete-calendar-accounts-icl4308d6701/mac>
1. Open [Dato](/dato), and click its item in the menu bar. The menu shows the calendar and your events of the next week.
1. If the Outlook events do not show, open the settings of Dato, click “Events”, click “Choose…” next to “Calendars”, and turn on the Outlook calendars.
}

To see the next meeting in the menu bar itself, turn on “Upcoming event & reminder in menu bar” in the same settings. [How to show your next meeting in the menu bar.](guide:show-next-meeting-menu-bar-mac)

Dato does not sync the events. macOS syncs them, so they show in Dato as soon as they show in the Calendar app. To open the events in Outlook, make Outlook the default calendar app: in the Calendar app, choose “Calendar › Settings”, click “General”, and choose Outlook in “Default calendar app”. Dato then has “Show in Outlook” for each event.

## Without an app

With the account in the Calendar app, the widgets of the Calendar app also show your Outlook events:

@Steps {
1. ++Control++-click the wallpaper, and choose “Edit Widgets…”.
1. Search for “Calendar”, and add a widget, like “Up Next”.
}

This is enough to see your next events on the desktop. Outlook for Mac shows a reminder before each meeting. Neither puts a calendar in the menu bar.
