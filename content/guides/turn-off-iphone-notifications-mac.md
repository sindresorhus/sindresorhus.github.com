@QuickAnswer {
	Open “[System Settings › Notifications](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Notifications-Settings.extension)”, click “Allow notifications from iPhone”, and turn off “Allow notifications from iPhone”. To turn them off and on again in one click, like before a presentation, add “iOS Notifications” to the menu of [Supercharge](/supercharge).
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge). In “General”, click the button next to “Shown menu items”.
1. Below “Toggles”, select “iOS Notifications”.
1. Click the Supercharge icon in the menu bar, and choose “iOS Notifications” to turn the notifications from your iPhone off or on. A check mark shows that they are on.
}

The “Set iOS Notifications Enabled” action of Supercharge does the same in the Shortcuts app, like in a shortcut that also turns on a Focus. This only works when your Mac has the “Allow notifications from iPhone” setting, which comes with iPhone Mirroring.

## Without an app

[Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/120684):

@Steps {
1. Open “[System Settings › Notifications](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Notifications-Settings.extension)”.
1. Click “Allow notifications from iPhone”.
1. Turn off “Allow notifications from iPhone”.
}

While the setting is on, the same page has a switch for each iPhone app, “Allow Live Activities from iPhone”, and “Play sounds for notifications from iPhone”. On your iPhone, the settings of each app in “Settings › Notifications” have “Show on Mac” too. A Focus pauses all notifications for a time.

This is enough when you change it now and then. Supercharge is faster when you turn them off and on often.
