@QuickAnswer {
	Open [Scratchpad](/scratchpad) on Apple Watch, tap the add button, and dictate, type, or scribble the text. It goes into one note that you also see on your iPhone and Mac. [Without an app](#without-an-app), the Notes app on Apple Watch can make a new note.
}

## With Scratchpad

@Steps {
1. Install [Scratchpad](/scratchpad) on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and turn on “iCloud Sync” in the settings on each.
1. Open Scratchpad on the watch. It shows your note.
1. Tap the add button, and dictate, type, or scribble the text. It goes at the end of the note.
}

Later, edit the note on your iPhone or Mac. On the watch, you can only add text, as watchOS cannot edit text with more than one line. To put new text at the start of the note, turn on “Prepend Text Instead of Append” in the settings of the watch app.

@Tips {
- Add Scratchpad to a watch face as a complication, so the note is one tap away.
- To send the note, tap the “…” button at the top, and tap “Share Text”.
}

## Without an app

The Notes app on Apple Watch shows the notes of your iPhone ([Apple’s guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apdc6fb0a03f/watchos)):

@Steps {
1. Open the Notes app on Apple Watch.
1. Tap the new note button, and dictate or scribble the text.
}

It is free, and good if you keep your notes in the Notes app. Like Scratchpad, it cannot edit a note on the watch. Each thought becomes a new note, while Scratchpad keeps them in one note, so you do not need to find them later.
