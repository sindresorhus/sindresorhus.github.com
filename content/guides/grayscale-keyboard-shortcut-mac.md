@QuickAnswer {
	In the “Shortcuts” settings of [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md), set a shortcut for “Grayscale mode”, and press it to make the screen black and white. Press it again for the colors. Without an app, set the color filter of macOS to “Grayscale”, and press ++Option+Command+F5++ to turn it on and off.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md), and click “Shortcuts”.
1. In the “Toggle feature” section, click the field next to “Grayscale mode”, and press the keyboard shortcut that you want.
1. Press the shortcut. The screen becomes black and white. Press it again to get the colors back.
}

You can also turn on “Grayscale Mode” in the Supercharge menu. In the Shortcuts app, the “Set Grayscale Mode” action of Supercharge turns it on, off, or toggles it, for example in an automation that turns the screen gray in the evening, so the Mac is less interesting before bed.

## Without an app

macOS has a Grayscale color filter ([Apple’s guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchl11ddd4b3/mac)):

@Steps {
1. Open “System Settings › Accessibility › Display”, and turn on “Color filters”.
1. In “Filter type”, choose “Grayscale”.
1. To turn it on and off later, press ++Option+Command+F5++ (or press Touch ID three times), select “Color Filters”, and click “Done”.
}

When “Color Filters” is the only checked feature in “System Settings › Accessibility › Shortcut”, the keys turn the filter on and off at once, without the panel ([Apple’s guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp2975/mac)). This is free and works well. Supercharge lets you choose the keys, and keeps the Accessibility Shortcuts for other features.
