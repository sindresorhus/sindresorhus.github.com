@QuickAnswer {
	Add the Reminders widget to the Home Screen to see a list of your reminders. To see only the one task that you must do now, add the “Task” widget of [One Task](/one-task), and tap it when the task is done.
}

## With One Task

@Steps {
1. Open [One Task](/one-task), and add the tasks that you want to do today.
1. Go to the Home Screen. Touch and hold the background until the apps begin to jiggle, tap “Edit”, then tap “Add Widget”.
1. Find One Task, tap it, swipe to the size that you want, and tap “Add Widget”. Tap “Done”.
}

The widget shows one task at a time. Tap the widget to complete the task, and the next task shows. One Task is not a full to-do app: it works best with only the tasks for today. To show the task on the Lock Screen too, see [how to put a to-do on the Lock Screen](guide:to-do-lock-screen-iphone).

## Without an app

The Reminders app has Home Screen widgets:

@Steps {
1. Touch and hold the Home Screen background, tap “Edit”, then tap “Add Widget”.
1. Find Reminders, tap it, choose a size, and tap “Add Widget”.
1. Touch and hold the widget, tap “Edit Widget”, and choose the list to show.
}

The widget shows the reminders of the list. This is the better way when you want to see many tasks, or when your tasks are already in Reminders.
