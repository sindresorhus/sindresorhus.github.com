@QuickAnswer {
	Select the files in Finder, and press ++Option+Command+⌫++. Finder asks you to confirm, and then deletes them without moving them to the Trash. This is like ++Shift+Delete++ on Windows. To do it from the right-click menu, use “Delete Immediately…” from [Supercharge](/supercharge).
}

You cannot get the files back from the Trash after this, so use it only for files that you are sure about, like a large download that you do not need.

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), click “Tweaks”, and click “Configure…” next to “Context menu items”.
1. In the “Main Menu” tab, turn on “Delete Immediately…”.
1. Right-click the files or folders in Finder, and choose “Delete Immediately…”.
1. Click “Delete”.
}

The right-click items cannot show in synced folders, like iCloud Drive and Dropbox.

## Without an app

**Keyboard shortcut:** Select the files in Finder, and press ++Option+Command+⌫++. Then confirm.

**From the Trash:** Move the files to the Trash with ++Command+⌫++, open the Trash, right-click a file, and choose “Delete Immediately…”. Then click “Delete”. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1093/mac>

The keyboard shortcut is all that you need if you remember it. Supercharge puts the command in the right-click menu, where you can see it.
