@QuickAnswer {
	[Supercharge](/supercharge) copies a link to a note: select the note in Notes, and press a keyboard shortcut, like ++Option+C++. Paste the link in Reminders, Calendar, or a document, and click it to open the note. [Without an app](#without-an-app), Notes can link from one note to another, but it cannot copy a link to use in other apps.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Notes” section, choose a keyboard shortcut for “Copy note link”: ++Control+C++, ++Option+C++, or ++Option+Shift+C++.
1. In Notes, select one or more notes in the list, and press the shortcut. You can also click the Supercharge icon in the menu bar, and choose “Copy Note Link”, which shows while Notes is in front.
1. The first time, give Supercharge Full Disk Access, and allow it to control Notes.
1. Paste the link. Its text is the title of the note.
}

The link opens the note in Notes on a Mac that has the note. To copy a link to an email, see [how to copy a link to an email in Mail](guide:copy-link-to-email-mac).

## Without an app

Notes can link from one note to another ([Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/notes/apde615d29c2/mac)):

@Steps {
1. In the note where you want the link, type `>>`.
1. Type the start of the title of the other note, and choose it.
}

“Edit › Add Link” does the same, and can also link to a section of a note. These links work only in Notes. To open a note from another app, search for its title with Spotlight.
