@QuickAnswer {
	Click [Recordia](/recordia) in the menu bar, choose “Microphone + System Audio”, and choose “Record” when the call starts. It records your voice and the voices of the others to one audio file, in any call app. [Without an app](#without-an-app), Zoom records a meeting on your computer when the host allows it, and a screen recording can include the sound of the Mac.
}

Tell the others that you record the call, and ask if they agree.

## With Recordia

@Steps {
1. Click the [Recordia](/recordia) icon in the menu bar, and below “Audio Source”, choose “Microphone + System Audio”.
1. Below “Input Device”, choose the microphone that you use in the call.
1. When the call starts, click the icon, and choose “Record”.
1. When the call ends, click the icon, and choose “Stop”. Then choose where to save the recording.
}

The system audio is all the sound that plays on your Mac, so Recordia records the call in Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or a call in the browser. [More about recording audio.](guide:record-audio-mac)

@Tips {
- If the others are too loud or too quiet in the recording, change “System audio volume” in the settings of Recordia.
- To get the text of the call, install [Aiko](/aiko), and choose “Previous Recording › Transcribe with Aiko” in the menu of Recordia. [How to transcribe audio to text.](guide:transcribe-audio-to-text-mac)
}

## Without an app

- **Zoom:** The Zoom app records a meeting on your computer. The host can record, and a participant can ask the host for permission to record. <source:https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0063640>
- **Google Meet:** Meet records a meeting only on some Google Workspace editions and Google One plans, and the recording goes to the Google Drive of the organizer. <source:https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308681>
- **Phone calls:** The Phone app on Mac records a phone call with “Call Recording”, where it is available in your language and region. Both people hear a notice. <source:https://support.apple.com/guide/phoneapp/phn4828a6/mac>
- **Screen recording:** On macOS 27 or later, press ++Shift+Command+5++, choose “Record Entire Screen”, and in “Options”, choose “Include System Audio” and a microphone. Then click “Record”. It saves a video, not an audio file.

The call apps record only when the host or the plan allows it. Recordia records any call to an audio file.
