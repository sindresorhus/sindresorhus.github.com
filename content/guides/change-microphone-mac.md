@QuickAnswer {
	Open “[System Settings › Sound](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Sound-Settings.extension)”, click “Input”, and select the microphone. To change it from the menu bar, turn on “Show input devices in the Sound menu bar item menu” in [Supercharge](/supercharge), or set a keyboard shortcut for “Cycle audio input device”.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Menu Bar” section, turn on “Show input devices in the Sound menu bar item menu”.
1. Click Sound in the menu bar. The microphones show below the output devices. Select one.
}

If Sound is not in the menu bar, open “[System Settings › Menu Bar](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.ControlCenter-Settings.extension)”, and set Sound to “Always Show in Menu Bar”.

For a keyboard shortcut, click “Shortcuts” in the settings, and set one for “Cycle audio input device”. Each press goes to the next microphone. [“Cycle audio output device” does the same for the speakers.](guide:switch-audio-output-shortcut-mac) To turn the microphone off and on, see [how to mute the microphone with a shortcut](guide:mute-microphone-shortcut-mac).

## Without an app

[Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp2567/mac):

@Steps {
1. Open “[System Settings › Sound](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Sound-Settings.extension)”.
1. Click “Input”, and select the microphone in the list.
1. Drag the “Input volume” slider if the sound is too loud or too quiet.
}

The microphone that you select is used by all apps that use the default microphone. Many video call apps, like Zoom and FaceTime, also have a microphone menu of their own. The settings are enough when you change the microphone now and then. Supercharge is faster when you switch many times a day, like between a headset and the microphone of the Mac.
