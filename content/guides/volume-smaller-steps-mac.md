@QuickAnswer {
	Hold ++Option+Shift++ while you press a volume key. The volume changes in smaller steps. To get smaller steps without holding keys, use [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md).
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md), and give it the access that it asks for in [“Device Control and Data Access”](/apps/faq#device-control-access).
1. Open the Supercharge settings, and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Keyboard” section, set “Volume key step size” to “Half” or “Quarter”.
1. Press a volume key. The volume changes in smaller steps.
}

“Brightness key step size” does the same for the brightness keys. Hold ++Option++ while you press a key to get a normal step. It may not work with some keyboards that are not from Apple.

## Without an app

- **Volume:** Hold ++Option+Shift++, and press the volume up or down key.
- **Brightness:** Hold ++Option+Shift++, and press the brightness up or down key.

[Apple’s keyboard shortcuts.](https://support.apple.com/102650) This is enough when you only sometimes need a small change. Supercharge is for when you want small steps each time, like with headphones, where a normal step is loud.
