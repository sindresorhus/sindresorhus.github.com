@QuickAnswer {
	By default, your Mac saves screenshots on the desktop. To see where yours go, press ++Shift+Command+5++, and click “Options”: the place with a check mark below “Save to” is the folder. To get your newest screenshots from the menu bar, add that folder to [Folder Peek](/folder-peek), and sort it by date.
}

## With Folder Peek

@Steps {
1. Make a folder for your screenshots, like a “Screenshots” folder in Pictures. Press ++Shift+Command+5++, click “Options”, choose “Other Location…”, and choose the folder.
1. Click the [Folder Peek](/folder-peek) icon in the menu bar, choose “Add Folder…”, choose the folder, and click “Add”.
1. Click the folder in the menu bar, open the submenu of the first item (the name of the folder), and choose “Configure Folder…”.
1. Set “Sort order” to “Date Created”, and “Max items” to a small number, like 20.
}

Click the folder in the menu bar to see your newest screenshots first. Drag a screenshot from the menu into an email or a chat, or click it to open it. To have the screenshot in the folder at once, turn off “Show Floating Thumbnail” in “Options”.

## Without an app

- **Find the folder:** The “Save to” place in “Options” of ++Shift+Command+5++, as in the answer above. <source:https://support.apple.com/102646> To change it, see [how to change where screenshots are saved](guide:change-screenshot-location-mac).
- **Search:** Press ++Command+Space++, and type “Screenshot”. The names of screenshots start with “Screenshot” and have the date and the time.
- **Dock:** Drag the folder to the right side of the Dock. ++Control++-click it, and set “Sort by” to “Date Added”. Then click it to see the newest screenshots.

The Dock and Spotlight are enough to find a screenshot now and then. Folder Peek is faster when you share screenshots many times a day.
