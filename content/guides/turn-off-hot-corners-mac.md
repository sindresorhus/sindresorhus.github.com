@QuickAnswer {
	Open “[System Settings › Desktop & Dock](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Desktop-Settings.extension)”, click “Hot Corners…”, and choose “-” for each corner. To turn all hot corners off for a time and get them back later, like for a game, choose “Hot Corners” in the menu of [Supercharge](/supercharge).
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Click the [Supercharge](/supercharge) icon in the menu bar.
1. Choose “Hot Corners”. All hot corners are off, and the check mark goes away.
1. Choose “Hot Corners” again to get back the actions that you had in each corner.
}

For a keyboard shortcut, open the Supercharge settings, click “Shortcuts”, and set one for “Hot corners” in the “Toggle feature” section. Hold ++Option++ while the menu is open, and choose “Configure Hot Corners…” to change the actions.

## Without an app

[Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp3000/mac):

@Steps {
1. Open “[System Settings › Desktop & Dock](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Desktop-Settings.extension)”, and click “Hot Corners…”.
1. For each corner that you do not want, choose “-” in its menu.
1. Click “Done”.
}

If the corners start actions by accident, but you still want them, hold ++Command++, ++Shift++, ++Option++, or ++Control++ while you choose the action. The corner then works only while you hold that key. This is the best fix for most people. Supercharge is for when you want all corners off for a time, and back with one click.
