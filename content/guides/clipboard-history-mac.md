@QuickAnswer {
	On macOS 26 or later, press ++Command+Space++ to open Spotlight, and then ++Command+4++. Spotlight shows what you copied before. To open the clipboard history with one keyboard shortcut, like ++Control+Command+V++, set it in [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md).
}

## With Supercharge

The built-in history needs two shortcuts, one after the other. Supercharge opens it directly:

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md), and click “Shortcuts”.
1. In the “Toggle specific Spotlight tab” section, click the field next to “Clipboard”, and press the keyboard shortcut that you want, like ++Control+Command+V++.
1. Press the shortcut in any app. Spotlight opens with the clipboard history.
}

The same section has shortcuts for the “Files” and “Actions” tabs of Spotlight. Supercharge does not keep a history of its own: it opens the one of macOS.

## Without an app

@Steps {
1. Press ++Command+Space++ to open Spotlight.
1. Press ++Command+4++, or click the Clipboard button to the right of the search field. The first time, Spotlight asks to show results from the clipboard: allow it.
1. Type to search the items that you copied, and select the one that you want. Press ++Command+C++ to copy it again, and paste it where you need it.
}

To erase the history, click the More button to the right of the search field, and choose “Clear History”. [Apple’s instructions.](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchl40d5b86b/mac)

The history is not kept forever, and private text that you copy can show in it. On macOS 15 and earlier, there is no clipboard history: macOS remembers only the last thing that you copied, and you need a clipboard manager app for more.

To see all the formats of what is on the clipboard now, see [how to see what is on the clipboard](see-clipboard-mac.md).
