@QuickAnswer {
	macOS has no setting to turn off the slide between desktops. In the “Shortcuts” settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), set keyboard shortcuts for “Switch to next space” and “Switch to previous space”. They switch at once, without the slide. [Without an app](#without-an-app), turn on “Reduce motion” in the Accessibility settings.
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](/supercharge), and click “Shortcuts”.
1. In the “Action” section, set a shortcut for “Switch to next space” and for “Switch to previous space”.
1. Press a shortcut to go to the desktop next to the current one.
}

In the Shortcuts app, the “Switch Space” action of Supercharge does the same, with “Next” or “Previous”.

Supercharge sends the switch to macOS like a fast swipe. If macOS does not accept it, Supercharge uses the normal shortcut of macOS, which has the slide.

## Without an app

Turn on “Reduce motion” in “[System Settings › Accessibility › Motion](x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.Accessibility-Settings.extension)” ([Apple’s guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlc03f57a1/mac)). macOS then shows less motion when you switch desktops with ++Control+▶++ or ++Control+◀++, or with a swipe.

This is free, and it can be enough if the slide makes you feel sick. But the switch still takes a moment, and “Reduce motion” also changes other animations of macOS, like the ones of Liquid Glass.
