@QuickAnswer {
	In Finder, ++Return++ renames the selected file. Press ++Command+O++ or ++Command+▼++ to open it. To open files with ++Return++, like with Enter on Windows, use [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md).
}

## With Supercharge

@Steps {
1. Open the settings of [Supercharge](../apps/supercharge/index.md), and click “Tweaks”.
1. In the “Finder” section, turn on “Open files with Return key”.
1. Select a file in Finder, and press ++Return++. The file opens.
}

To rename a file, press ++Shift+Return++ or ++F2++.

## Without an app

- **Open:** Select the file, and press ++Command+O++ or ++Command+▼++. Or double-click it.
- **Rename:** Select the file, and press ++Return++.

macOS has no setting to make ++Return++ open files. ++Command+O++ is easy to learn. Supercharge is for when your fingers expect Enter to open, like when you also use Windows.
